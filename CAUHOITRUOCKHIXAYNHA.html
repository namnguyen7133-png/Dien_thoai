1. Pham vi cong viec cua Nha thau

Bat buoc lam ro

Chon ro goi (nhan cong / vat tu tho / hoan thien / tron goi). Ghi danh sach hang muc cu the neu tron goi (bep, son, thiet bi ve sinh, vach trang tri, cong rao...)

2. Ban ve xay dung da day du chua?

Kiem tra kien truc, ket cau, dien, nuoc; yeu cau ho so chi tiet thi cong truoc khi ky.

3. Ai la nguoi giam sat cong trinh?

Du tron goi, gia chu nen phan dinh nguoi giam sat: gia chu, nguoi than co chuyen mon, hay giam sat doc lap.

4. Thanh toan, bao hanh, xu ly phat sinh

Lam ro lich thanh toan theo tien do, dieu kien bao hanh, & co che xu ly phat sinh (cach tinh, trach nhiem).

5. Vat tu chat luong & thuong hieu

Yeu cau ro chung loai vat tu tho va hoan thien, cung cap danh sach & chung chi chat luong.

Tong hop - Tao ban tom tat hop dong

Sau khi da luu tung phan, bam "Tao tom tat" de sinh van ban tom tat nhanh (co the tai ve hoac in).
