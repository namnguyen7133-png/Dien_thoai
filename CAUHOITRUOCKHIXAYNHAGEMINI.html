2. ✅ Danh Sach 5 Cau Hoi Bat Buoc Phai Lam Ro

❓ 1. Pham vi Cong viec cua Nha thau la gi? [01:11]

Can lam ro:

  • Xac dinh ro goi thau: Nhan cong, Vat tu tho, Hoan thien, hay Tron goi (full noi that)?
  • Hang muc nao dA BAO GOM trong goi tron goi: bep, son nuoc, thiet bi ve sinh, vach trang tri, mat tien, den, cong rao, cua, khung bao ve?
❓ 2. Ban ve Xay dung da day du chua? [03:14]

Ho so can phai bao gom toi thieu 4 phan chinh:

  • Kien truc: Kich thuoc, cao do, cong nang mat bang, vi tri cua.
  • Ket cau: Chi tiet ve mong, coc, dam mong, do day nen, cot thep.
  • dien: Vi tri day dien, thiet bi, CP (cau dao), cong tac, o cam.
  • Nuoc: He thong cap thoat nuoc, chong tham, ket cau mai.
❓ 3. Ai la nguoi Giam sat Cong trinh? [05:19]

Can xac dinh ro nguoi giam sat chinh:

  • Gia chu tu giam sat (neu co kien thuc va thoi gian).
  • Nguoi than co chuyen mon.
  • Khuyen nghi: Thue don vi Giam sat doc lap de kiem soat chat luong, an toan va giai quyet ky thuat.
❓ 4. Thanh toan, Bao hanh va Xu ly Phat sinh nhu the nao? [06:36]

Lam ro cac yeu to phap ly va tai chinh:

  • Quy dinh cu the ve cac dot Thanh toan.
  • Chi tiet ve thoi gian va pham vi Bao hanh.
  • Cach xu ly Phat sinh (tang/giam): Trach nhiem do nhu cau gia chu hay su co ngoai cong trinh?
  • Luu y: Du tru 10-20% chi phi tong cho phat sinh.
❓ 5. Vat tu Chat luong va Thuong hieu nao duoc su dung? [08:04]

Yeu cau Nha thau cung cap:

  • Danh sach vat tu tho (cat, thep, gach, be tong thuong pham hay tron tay).
  • Danh sach vat tu hoan thien kem Thuong hieu (gach, thiet bi dien/nuoc, tran, cua).
  • Chung chi chat luong cua vat tu.
