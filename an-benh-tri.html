🔥 THUC dON HOA VUONG

🔥

Trieu chung Hoa vuong:

Bui tri sung nong do, chay mau tuoi, tao bon, khat nuoc, mat do, nuoc tieu vang

DUNG THUY dE KHAC HOA

  • Bua sang: Chao dau den + Trung luoc
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Canh rau ma + Ca hap
  • Bua toi: Mien dong + dau phu sot ca + Rau luoc
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Daflon 500mg: 2 vien x 3 lan/ngay (cam mau, giam sung)
  • Paracetamol 500mg: 1 vien khi dau nhieu
  • Vitamin C 1000mg: 1 vien/ngay (tang suc ben mach mau)

DUNG KIM dE TIET HOA

  • Bua sang: Ngu coc nguyen hat + Sua chua
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Canh bi dao + Thit ga hap
  • Bua toi: Sup bi do + Sandwich ca hoi
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Macrogol 10g: 1-2 goi/ngay (chong tao bon)
  • Kem boi tri: Proctolog hoac Preparation H (giam ngua, sung)
  • Vitamin E 400 IU: 1 vien/ngay (duong am, chong viem)
🔁 THAY THE DE MUA:
  • Gao lut → Gao luc tim than (de mua hon)
  • Rau ma → Rau diep ca (cung tac dung thanh nhiet)
  • dau den → dau xanh khong vo (de tieu hon)

🍽 THUC dON 1 NGAY CHO HOA VUONG

🌅 BUA SANG (7-8h)
  • Chao dau den (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Trung ga luoc (1 qua)
  • Nuoc ep dua chuot (150ml)
☀ BUA TRUA (12-13h)
  • Com gao lut (100g)
  • Ca diec hap (100g)
  • Canh mong toi (200ml)
  • Rau muong luoc (150g)
🌆 BUA XE (16h)
  • Sua chua khong duong (1 hop)
  • Thanh long do (1/2 qua)
🌙 BUA TOI (19-20h)
  • Mien dong nau thit nac (tuong duong 100g com)
  • dau phu sot ca chua (100g)
  • Canh rau den (200ml)

🌊 THUC dON THUY VUONG

🌊

Trieu chung Thuy vuong:

Troi ret lanh, nguoi on lanh, chay nuoc mui trong, tieu chay, bui tri sung lanh

DUNG THO dE KHAC THUY

  • Bua sang: Chao gao nep + Thit ga xe
  • Bua trua: Com trang (100g) + Thit bo xao + Canh khoai tay
  • Bua toi: Com trang (100g) + Ca kho + Rau cu ham
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Loperamide 2mg: 1-2 vien khi tieu chay (khong qua 8mg/ngay)
  • Vitamin B Complex: 1 vien/ngay (tang chuyen hoa)
  • Men vi sinh: Probiotic 1 goi/ngay (can bang he tieu hoa)

DUNG HOA dE TIET THUY

  • Bua sang: Banh mi nuong + Trung op la
  • Bua trua: Com trang (100g) + Canh rau den + Thit ga rang
  • Bua toi: Mi gao + Thit heo xao + Rau xao toi
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Khang sinh (neu co nhiem khuan): Theo chi dinh bac si
  • Vitamin D3 1000 IU: 1 vien/ngay (tang mien dich)
  • Thuoc giam dau: Ibuprofen 400mg khi can (khang viem)
🔁 THAY THE DE MUA:
  • Gao nep → Gao te thuong (de tieu hon)
  • Khoai tay → Khoai lang hoac Bi do (cung nhom)
  • Mi gao → Bun tuoi hoac Pho kho (de tim hon)

🍽 THUC dON 1 NGAY CHO THUY VUONG

🌅 BUA SANG (7-8h)
  • Chao gao nep thit ga (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Banh mi nuong (1 lat)
  • Tra gung mat ong (200ml)
☀ BUA TRUA (12-13h)
  • Com trang (100g)
  • Thit bo xao hanh tay (80g thit)
  • Canh khoai tay ca rot (200ml)
  • Rau cai luoc (150g)
🌆 BUA XE (16h)
  • Khoai lang hap (1 cu nho)
  • Sua am (200ml)
🌙 BUA TOI (19-20h)
  • Com trang (100g)
  • Ca thu kho (1 khuc)
  • Canh bi do (200ml)
  • dau cove luoc (150g)

⚔ THUC dON KIM VUONG

⚔

Trieu chung Kim vuong:

Da kho, tao bon nang, ho khan, bui tri kho dau, tam trang buon ba, dau lung

DUNG HOA dE KHAC KIM

  • Bua sang: Chao hat sen + Trung chan
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Tim heo xao + Canh rau ngot
  • Bua toi: Sup bi do + Thit ga hap + Salad rau
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Macrogol 10g: 1-2 goi/ngay (lam mem phan)
  • Vitamin E 400 IU: 1 vien/ngay (duong am niem mac)
  • Docusate sodium 100mg: 1 vien/ngay (chat lam mem phan)

DUNG THUY dE TIET KIM

  • Bua sang: Ngu coc + Sua tuoi
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Canh mong toi + Ca kho
  • Bua toi: Chao ca + Rau cu hap + dau phu
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Dau khoang: 15-30ml/ngay (boi tron ruot)
  • Vitamin C 1000mg: 1 vien/ngay (chong oxy hoa)
  • Kem duong am: Boi vung hau mon 2 lan/ngay
🔁 THAY THE DE MUA:
  • Hat sen → dau xanh khong vo (de nau hon)
  • Tim heo → Thit heo nac hoac Uc ga (de mua hon)
  • Ngu coc nguyen hat → Yen mach an lien (tien loi)

🍽 THUC dON 1 NGAY CHO KIM VUONG

🌅 BUA SANG (7-8h)
  • Chao dau xanh (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Trung chan (1 qua)
  • Sua tuoi am (200ml)
☀ BUA TRUA (12-13h)
  • Com gao lut (100g)
  • Thit ga hap (100g)
  • Canh mong toi nau cua (200ml)
  • Rau lang luoc (150g)
🌆 BUA XE (16h)
  • Chuoi tieu chin (1 qua)
  • Nuoc ep le (150ml)
🌙 BUA TOI (19-20h)
  • Chao ca loc (tuong duong 100g com)
  • dau phu sot ca (100g)
  • Canh bau nau tom (200ml)
  • Bi xanh hap (150g)

🌳 THUC dON MOC VUONG

🌳

Trieu chung Moc vuong:

Cang thang, de cau gat, dau dau, chuot rut, bui tri co that dau nhoi, phan dau cung duoi nat

DUNG KIM dE KHAC MOC

  • Bua sang: Chao gao lut + Cu cai trang ham
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Thit ga hap gung + Su hao xao
  • Bua toi: Mien gao + Ca thu kho + Rau cai chip
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Spasmaverine (Alverine): 1 vien khi dau co that
  • Magnesium 250mg: 1 vien/ngay (gian co)
  • Vitamin B6 50mg: 1 vien/ngay (ho tro than kinh)

DUNG HOA dE TIET MOC

  • Bua sang: Yen mach + Hat chia + Mat ong
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Tim lon xao can tay + Canh kho qua
  • Bua toi: Com gao lut (100g) + Ca hoi ap chao + Rau bina
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Thuoc an than nhe: Rotunda hoac Seduxen (theo chi dinh)
  • Vitamin C 1000mg: 1 vien/ngay (giam stress oxy hoa)
  • Melatonin 3mg: Truoc khi ngu (neu mat ngu)
🔁 THAY THE DE MUA:
  • Su hao → Cu cai trang (de tim hon)
  • Can tay → Rau can nuoc (pho bien hon)
  • Rau bina → Rau muong hoac Rau ngot

🍽 THUC dON 1 NGAY CHO MOC VUONG

🌅 BUA SANG (7-8h) - Dung Kim khac Moc
  • Chao gao lut cu cai trang (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Trung ga luoc (1 qua)
  • Nuoc ep le (150ml)
☀ BUA TRUA (12-13h) - Dung Hoa tiet Moc
  • Com gao lut (100g)
  • Tim lon xao can tay (80g tim + 100g can)
  • Canh kho qua nhoi thit (1 qua nho)
  • Rau muong luoc (150g)
🌆 BUA XE (16h) - Nhe nhang
  • Sua chua khong duong + hat chia (1 hop 100g)
  • Chuoi tieu chin (1 qua ≈ 80g)
🌙 BUA TOI (19-20h) - Duong Am
  • Chao ca loc + dau xanh (tuong duong 100g com)
  • dau phu sot ca chua (100g)
  • Canh rau ngot (200ml)
📝 LUU Y dAC BIET CHO MOC VUONG:
  • Tranh do chua nhieu (dua muoi, cam chua) - lam Moc cang vuong
  • Tranh ruou bia, ca phe - kich thich Can hoa boc len
  • Ngu truoc 23h - gio Ty (Thuy) duong Can (Moc)
  • Tap tho sau, yoga nhe nhang de thu gian

⛰ THUC dON THO VUONG

⛰

Trieu chung Tho vuong:

Nguoi nang ne, day bung, an khong tieu, phan nhao dinh, bui tri sa xuong nang, co nhao

DUNG MOC dE KHAC THO

  • Bua sang: Chao yen mach + Hat chia
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Ca diec hap + Rau can xao
  • Bua toi: Mien gao + Thit bo xao mang + Rau muong
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Men tieu hoa: Pancreatin 1-2 vien sau an
  • Domperidone 10mg: 1 vien truoc an 30 phut
  • Simethicone 125mg: 1-2 vien khi day bung

DUNG KIM dE TIET THO

  • Bua sang: Sup bi do hat ke
  • Bua trua: Com gao lut (100g) + Thit ga ham hat sen + Cu cai muoi
  • Bua toi: Chao gao lut + Tom hap + Rau lang luoc
THAY THE TAY Y:
  • Thuoc nhuan trang: Macrogol 10g 1 goi/ngay
  • Probiotic: 1 goi/ngay (can bang he vi sinh)
  • Vitamin B1 100mg: 1 vien/ngay (kich thich tieu hoa)
🔁 THAY THE DE MUA:
  • Hat ke → Hat sen kho hoac dau xanh
  • Cu cai muoi → Ca phao muoi hoac Dua chuot muoi
  • Mang → Gia do hoac Nam (de tieu hon)

🍽 THUC dON 1 NGAY CHO THO VUONG

🌅 BUA SANG (7-8h) - Dung Moc khac Tho
  • Chao yen mach + hat chia (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Ca diec hap gung (1 con nho ≈ 80g)
  • Nuoc ep buoi (150ml)
☀ BUA TRUA (12-13h) - Dung Kim tiet Tho
  • Com gao lut (100g)
  • Thit ga ham hat sen (80g thit + 30g hat sen)
  • Cu cai muoi chua nhe (100g)
  • Rau cai xao toi (150g)
🌆 BUA XE (16h) - Thanh nhiet
  • Tra gung chanh mat ong (200ml)
  • du du chin (100g)
🌙 BUA TOI (19-20h) - Nhe nhang
  • Sup bi do hat ke (tuong duong 100g com)
  • Rau lang luoc (150g)
  • Tau hu ky kho nam (100g)
📝 LUU Y dAC BIET CHO THO VUONG:
  • Tranh do ngot nhieu (banh keo, nuoc ngot) - lam Tho cang vuong
  • Tranh do chien ran, nhieu dau mo - gay kho tieu
  • An cham, nhai ky, khong an qua no
  • di bo nhe nhang 30 phut sau an toi
  • Massage bung theo chieu kim dong ho truoc khi ngu
