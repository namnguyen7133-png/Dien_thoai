Tu Bai Toan Co den Ung Dung Thuc Te

Bai toan "dem nguoi lam nha" la gi?

Bai toan co voi noi dung: "3 nguoi an chung 1 bat com, 4 nguoi an chung 1 dia ca muoi, tong co 301 bat dia. Hoi co bao nhieu nguoi?"

day khong chi la bai toan giai tri ma chua dung nguyen ly phan bo ti le co the ung dung trong nhieu linh vuc thuc te.

Nguyen ly cot loi

Bai toan dua tren phuong trinh ti le: A/x + B/y = Tong

Trong do:

  • A: So don vi loai 1 (bat com)
  • B: So don vi loai 2 (dia ca)
  • x: So nguoi dung chung 1 don vi loai 1
  • y: So nguoi dung chung 1 don vi loai 2

Cong thuc tong quat: N/x + N/y = Tong so don vi

💡

Tai sao bai toan nay co ung dung thuc te?

Trong cuoc song, chung ta thuong xuyen gap cac tinh huong can phan bo tai nguyen theo ti le:

  • Phan chia chi phi theo nhom
  • Tinh toan nguyen vat lieu cho san xuat
  • Quy hoach khong gian su dung
  • Lap ke hoach nhan su va trang thiet bi

Ung Dung Thuc Te Trong Cuoc Song

1. Quan ly nha hang, quan an

Tinh huong: Tinh so ban, ghe can thiet khi biet so khach va cach sap xep.

Vi du: Ban 4 nguoi va ban 6 nguoi, tong can phuc vu 100 khach voi 25 ban.

Giai phap: Ap dung nguyen ly bai toan de tinh so ban moi loai.

2. To chuc su kien, hoi nghi

Tinh huong: Bo tri cho ngoi voi ban tron (10 nguoi/ban) va ban dai (8 nguoi/ban).

Vi du: Can sap xep 200 dai bieu vao 22 ban.

Giai phap: Dung cong thuc tuong tu de tinh so ban moi loai can chuan bi.

3. Quan ly van phong, coworking space

Tinh huong: Phan bo khong gian lam viec: phong hop 4 nguoi va phong lam viec nhom 6 nguoi.

Vi du: Cong ty co 50 nhan vien can 12 phong lam viec.

Giai phap: Tinh so phong moi loai de toi uu khong gian.

4. Lap ke hoach san xuat

Tinh huong: Tinh nguyen vat lieu cho san pham co thanh phan ti le khac nhau.

Vi du: San xuat 2 loai banh: loai A can 200g bot/banh, loai B can 150g bot/banh, tong dung 5kg bot cho 30 cai banh.

Giai phap: Ap dung cong thuc de tinh so banh moi loai.

5. Quan ly lop hoc, khoa dao tao

Tinh huong: Phan nhom hoc tap voi quy mo khac nhau.

Vi du: Lop 48 hoc sinh chia thanh nhom 3 va nhom 4, tong co 13 nhom.

Giai phap: Tinh so nhom moi loai de phan chia hop ly.

Huong dan su dung

2. Cac thong so se tu dong cap nhat theo tinh huong

3. dieu chinh thong so neu can thiet

4. Nhan "Tinh Toan" de xem ket qua va cac buoc giai

Tinh Huong Ung Dung Cu The

🎯 Tinh huong 1: Nha hang tiec cuoi

Thong tin: Can sap xep 150 khach vao cac ban tiec. Co 2 loai ban: ban 8 khach va ban 10 khach. Tong can 18 ban.

Bai toan: Tinh so ban moi loai can chuan bi.

🏢 Tinh huong 2: Coworking Space

Thong tin: Khong gian lam viec chung co 40 nguoi dang ky. Co 2 loai phong: phong 4 nguoi va phong 6 nguoi. Tong co 8 phong.

Bai toan: Tinh so phong moi loai can bo tri.

🏫 Tinh huong 3: Truong hoc

Thong tin: Lop hoc co 36 hoc sinh can chia nhom thao luan. Co 2 loai nhom: nhom 3 nguoi va nhom 5 nguoi. Tong co 9 nhom.

Bai toan: Tinh so nhom moi loai.

🏨 Tinh huong 4: Khach san

Thong tin: doan khach 80 nguoi can dat phong. Co 2 loai phong: phong doi (2 nguoi) va phong 3 nguoi. Tong can 30 phong.

Bai toan: Tinh so phong moi loai can dat.

Cac buoc giai quyet tinh huong thuc te

Xac dinh yeu to can tinh (so luong moi loai)
Xac dinh cac thong so: so nguoi/don vi moi loai, tong so nguoi, tong so don vi
Thiet lap phuong trinh dua tren nguyen ly bai toan goc
Giai phuong trinh de tim so luong moi loai
Kiem tra lai ket qua va dieu chinh neu can

Nguyen Ly Toan Hoc Va Ung Dung

Cong thuc tong quat tu bai toan

N/x + N/y = T

Trong do:

  • N: Tong so nguoi/so luong chung
  • x: So nguoi dung chung 1 don vi loai 1
  • y: So nguoi dung chung 1 don vi loai 2
  • T: Tong so don vi

Cong thuc giai: N = (T × x × y) / (x + y)

Mo rong cho nhieu loai hon

Bai toan co the mo rong cho 3 loai tro len:

N/x + N/y + N/z = T

Giai: N = T / (1/x + 1/y + 1/z)

Ung dung trong quan ly tai nguyen

Nguyen ly nay giup:

  • Toi uu hoa su dung tai nguyen: Phan bo hop ly de tranh lang phi
  • Lap ke hoach chinh xac: Tinh toan truoc nhu cau thuc te
  • Giai quyet van de phan bo: Khi co nhieu lua chon voi ti le khac nhau
  • Du bao nhu cau: Trong san xuat, dich vu, giao duc
🚀

Loi ich thuc te khi ap dung

1. Tiet kiem chi phi: Tranh mua thua hoac thieu nguyen vat lieu, trang thiet bi

2. Toi uu khong gian: Sap xep, bo tri hop ly khong gian su dung

3. Cai thien hieu qua: Phan bo nhan luc, thoi gian hop ly

4. Nang cao chat luong dich vu: dap ung dung nhu cau khach hang, nguoi su dung
