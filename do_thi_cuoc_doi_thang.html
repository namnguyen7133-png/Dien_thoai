Xac dinh dia chi cua thang Trong he thong lich A dong, dia chi cua cac thang am lich luon co dinh nhu sau: Thang 1: Dan Thang 2: Mao Thang 3: Thin Thang 4: Ty Thang 5: Ngo Thang 6: Mui Thang 7: Than Thang 8: Dau Thang 9: Tuat Thang 10: Hoi Thang 11: Ty Thang 12: Suu 2. Xac dinh Thien can cua thang Thien can cua thang 1 (thang Dan) duoc tinh dua theo Thien can cua nam. Sau khi co Thien can cua thang 1, cac thang tiep theo se tinh theo thu tu tuan hoan cua 10 Thien can (Giap, At, Binh, dinh, Mau, Ky, Canh, Tan, Nham, Quy). Quy tac tim Thien can thang 1 (thang Dan): Nam co Thien can la Giap hoac Ky: Thang 1 la Binh Dan. Nam co Thien can la At hoac Canh: Thang 1 la Mau Dan. Nam co Thien can la Binh hoac Tan: Thang 1 la Canh Dan. Nam co Thien can la dinh hoac Nham: Thang 1 la Nham Dan. Nam co Thien can la Mau hoac Quy: Thang 1 la Giap Dan. 3. Vi du minh hoa (Nam 2025 - At Ty) Nam 2025 co Thien can la At. Dua vao quy tac tren, nam At thi thang 1 la thang Mau Dan. Cac thang tiep theo cua nam 2025 se tinh noi tiep: Thang 2: Ky Mao Thang 3: Canh Thin Thang 4: Tan Ty ... (tuong tu cho den het 12 thang). Luu y: Viec tinh Can Chi thang bat buoc phai dua tren thang am lich. Neu ban sinh vao thang nhuan, Can Chi cua thang nhuan do van duoc tinh giong nhu thang chinh.
