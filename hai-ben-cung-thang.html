LAM CHU CUOC CHOI – TU DUY dOI BEN CUNG THANG (WIN‑WIN)

Nguyen tac cot loi: Khong chia tien truoc – chi chia sau khi da song sot.

1. Luat song con (ap dung cho moi phep tinh)

  • ✅ Hoa von truoc
  • ✅ Co dong tien duong
  • ✅ Co lai gop an toan
  • ❌ Khong dat → khong chia

2. Mo hinh chia doi – doi ben cung co loi

Ap dung khi hop tac (mat bang – von – cong suc).

Cong thuc Win‑Win chuan

Loi nhuan chia = Loi nhuan rong – Quy an toan

Moi ben = Loi nhuan chia × % thoa thuan

3. May tinh chia loi nhuan (chia doi / theo ty le)

Nguoi Hoa khong chia khi chua co quy an toan. Nguoi lam chu cuoc choi luon giu quyen kiem soat tien.

4. Ap dung Win‑Win vao cac phep tinh ban co

  • diem hoa von: Chi chia loi nhuan khi vuot ≥ 120% hoa von
  • Dong tien: Chi chia khi dong tien duong ≥ 2 chu ky
  • Lai gop: < 40% → khong chia, phai sua mo hinh
  • AOV: Thuong theo AOV tang them (khong theo doanh thu)
  • Doanh thu/m²: Ai toi uu m² tot hon → thuong them

5. Bai hoc lam chu cuoc choi

❝ Nguoi yeu hoi chia bao nhieu. Nguoi manh dat dieu kien moi duoc chia. ❞

Win‑Win khong phai la chia deu – ma la cung song, cung lon, khong cung chet.
