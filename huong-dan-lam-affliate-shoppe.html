Cach lay link san pham tren Shopee bang app & web nhanh nhat 2025

Tac gia: dinh Chung
Ngay dang: 23/04/2025

Viec lay hay sao chep link san pham Shopee tuong chung don gian nhung lai vo cung quan trong. Chi khi co duoc link dung, ban moi co the gan san pham trong Shopee Live, chia se cho ban be, lay ma giam gia hoac tao link tiep thi lien ket de kiem hoa hong. Vay ban co the lay link san pham Shopee o dau? Cach lay link tren ung dung va may tinh co gi khac nhau?

Trong bai viet nay, Phan mem ban hang da kenh Nhanh se huong dan ban cach lay link san pham tren Shopee mot cach nhanh chong, chinh xac, de ap dung, phu hop cho ca nguoi moi lan nguoi ban chuyen nghiep trong nam 2025.

1. Cac loai link san pham Shopee pho bien hien nay

Truoc khi bat tay vao huong dan cach lay link san pham, ban can hieu ro: khong phai link nao cung giong nhau.

Tren Shopee, cung la mot san pham, nhung tuy theo cach lay, thiet bi va muc dich su dung ma duong dan co the co hinh thuc, do dai, chuc nang va do hieu qua khac nhau.

1.1. Link goc (link day du tu thanh dia chi trinh duyet)

Link goc xuat hien khi ban mo mot san pham tren Shopee bang trinh duyet web (Chrome, Safari, Coc Coc,…). Link nay thuong dai, chua day du thong tin ve san pham, ten shop, ID san pham, phan loai, ma chien dich neu co nhu "https://shopee.vn/San-pham-gi-do-i.12345678.9876543210?sp_atk=abcxyz"

Uu diem: Link nay day du va minh bach, thich hop khi gui qua email, dung trong cac cong cu quang cao hoac tracking chi tiet.

Nhuoc diem: Vi kha dai nen neu khong duoc rut gon, chung co the gay roi mat va giam ty le click neu chia se tren mang xa hoi.

1.2. Link rut gon tu ung dung Shopee (shopee.vn/xxxx)

Khi ban chia se san pham tu app Shopee, he thong se tu dong tao ra mot link rut gon voi dinh dang: "https://shopee.vn/abcxyz"

Uu diem: Loai link nay la loai link pho bien nhat khi gui cho ban be qua Zalo, Messenger, SMS, hoac chia se tren story, caption,… Uu diem cua no la ngan gon, de nhin, de click.

Nhuoc diem: Kho theo doi hieu suat neu ban lam tiep thi lien ket, va doi khi khong hien thi day du thong tin phan loai san pham nhu link goc.

1.3. Link Affiliate (co gan ma theo doi & hoa hong)

Neu ban la nguoi lam Shopee Affiliate, hoac su dung cac nen tang nhu AccessTrade, Shopee KOL,… ban se duoc cap mot duong link rieng cho tung san pham kha dai va kem theo ma affiliate: "https://shope.ee/ABCxyzaf_click_lookback=7d&af_siteid=xyz123&utm_campaign=affiliate…" Link nay co kha nang do luong chinh xac luot click, chuyen doi, va ghi nhan hoa hong neu nguoi mua dat hang.

Luu y quan trong: Ban can luu y tao dung cach, neu lay sai link hoac gan sai ma chien dich, toan bo no luc tiep thi se khong duoc ghi nhan doanh thu.

Tom lai: Du ban la nguoi mua hang hay nha ban hang, nam ro cac loai link san pham Shopee se giup ban chia se dung cach, tiet kiem thoi gian va tang hieu qua cong viec.

2. Cach lay link san pham Shopee tren ung dung Shopee

Voi xu huong hien nay khi nguoi dung uu tien mua sam tren dien thoai, viec biet cach lay link san pham Shopee bang app la ky nang khong the thieu, nhat la khi ban thuong xuyen chia se san pham voi nguoi than, ban be, hoac ban hang qua Zalo, Facebook, TikTok,…

2.1. Huong dan tung buoc lay link tren app Shopee

  1. Buoc 1: Mo ung dung Shopee tren dien thoai (Android hoac iOS)
  2. Buoc 2: Tim den san pham ban muon chia se > Nhan vao san pham de mo trang chi tiet
  3. Buoc 3: Tai giao dien chi tiet san pham, ban se thay bieu tuong chia se (mui ten cong) o goc tren ben phai man hinh
  4. Buoc 4: Nhan vao bieu tuong chia se > Chon "Sao chep duong dan" hoac nhan "Luu anh" de chia se bang ma Qr
  5. Buoc 5: Dan lien ket vua sao chep vao noi ban muon chia se (tin nhan, caption, bai viet, bio,…)

2.2. Nhung luu y khi lay link Shopee tren ung dung

  • Link co the tu dong rut gon hoac gan ma chien dich neu ban la Shopee KOL/tiep thi lien ket (Affiliate).
  • Khi chia se sang cac nen tang nhu Zalo, Instagram,…Shopee se tao mot duong link rieng dang ngan, tuy nhien doi khi se bi ma hoa ngam khien ban khong theo doi duoc luot click neu khong kiem tra ky.
  • Link sao chep tu app thuong ngan gon va than thien, nhung khong phai luc nao cung phu hop cho muc dich tracking hay chay quang cao chuyen sau.

3. Cach lay link san pham Shopee bang trinh duyet Web (PC/Laptop)

Neu ban thuong xuyen su dung may tinh de lam viec, viet noi dung, quan ly gian hang Shopee hoac don gian la muon co duoc link san pham day du va de chinh sua, thi viec lay link truc tiep tren trinh duyet web la lua chon toi uu.

3.1. Cac buoc lay link san pham Shopee tren may tinh

  1. Buoc 1: Truy cap vao https://shopee.vn bang trinh duyet ban dang su dung (Chrome, Firefox, Coc Coc…)
  2. Buoc 2: Go tu khoa san pham vao o tim kiem
  3. Buoc 3: Nhan chon san pham de mo trang chi tiet san pham
  4. Buoc 4: Di chuyen chuot den thanh dia chi (URL) tren cung > Click chuot phai > Chon "Sao chep" (hoac nhan to hop phim Ctrl + C)
  5. Buoc 5: Dan lien ket vua sao chep vao noi ban muon chia se (caption, bai viet, bio,…)

4. Huong dan tao link tiep thi lien ket Shopee chuan Affiliate

Viec chia se link san pham Shopee don thuan giup ban gui duoc thong tin cho nguoi khac, nhung de kiem tien tu viec chia se ay, ban can hieu va ap dung dung cach tao link tiep thi lien ket (Affiliate).

4.1. Link tiep thi lien ket la gi? Vi sao can tao dung chuan?

Link tiep thi lien ket (Affiliate Link) la duong dan dac biet co gan ma theo doi rieng cho tung cong tac vien hoac KOL (nguoi anh huong). Khi ban chia se link nay va ai do mua hang qua do, ban se nhan duoc hoa hong tu Shopee.

Quan trong: Neu ban chi copy link san pham thong thuong tu app hoac web thi se khong duoc ghi nhan don hang, tuc la ban mat trang phan hoa hong. Do do, tao link Affiliate dung chuan la yeu to bat buoc neu ban lam tiep thi lien ket.

5. Nhung loi thuong gap khi lay hoac chia se link san pham Shopee

Viec lay link san pham tren Shopee tuong chung don gian, nhung tren thuc te lai co khong it nguoi dung ke ca nguoi ban hang lau nam hay nguoi lam Affiliate mac loi khi thao tac. Nhung loi nay khong chi gay gian doan trai nghiem chia se ma con anh huong truc tiep den hieu qua ban hang va hoa hong tiep thi.

5.1. Sao chep sai loai link khong ho tro tracking

Mot trong nhung loi pho bien nhat la copy nham link tu app Shopee roi dung lam link tiep thi. Cac link nay thuong la dang rut gon (shopee.vn/abcxyz), de chia se nhung khong chua ma tiep thi lien ket, dan den viec khong duoc ghi nhan don hang va khong co hoa hong.

5.2. Lay link khi chua dang nhap dung tai khoan Affiliate

Mot loi "ngo ngan nhung thuong gap" la viec tao link khi ban chua dang nhap vao tai khoan Affiliate hoac dang nhap nham tai khoan. Luc nay, du tao dung thao tac, link van khong ghi nhan cho ban.

5.5. Dung link dai gay kho chiu cho nguoi xem

Link goc tu Shopee thuong rat dai, chua nhieu ky tu phuc tap, gay mat tham my khi chia se tren mang xa hoi, bai viet hoac chat. Nguoi dung co the ngai click vao link qua dai vi so la spam hoac virus.

Goi y: Hay rut gon link, hoac gan link trong nut CTA, hinh anh, van ban co chen hyperlink de toi uu trai nghiem nguoi xem.
