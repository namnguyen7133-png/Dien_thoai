11 PHEP TINH "XUONG MAU" CUA NGUOI HOA

Kinh doanh khong phai la cam tinh - Hay tinh toan nhu nguoi Hoa de luon co lai

1. diem Hoa Von (Break-Even Point)

Cau hoi: "Mot ngay toi phai ban duoc bao nhieu bat pho thi moi bat dau co lai?"

Ket Qua diem Hoa Von

Ban can ban: 0 san pham de dat diem hoa von.

day la con so quan trong nhat, quyet dinh su song con cua quan.

Giai thich:

diem hoa von la so luong san pham ban can ban de trang trai tat ca chi phi (co dinh va bien doi).

Cong thuc: diem hoa von = Tong chi phi co dinh / (Gia ban trung binh 1 san pham - Chi phi bien doi trung binh 1 san pham)

2. Dong Tien (Cash Flow)

Cau hoi: "Tien trong ket co du de tra tien nha, luong nhan vien va tien hang thang sau khong?"

Ket Qua Dong Tien

Dong tien cua ban: 0 dong

Nguoi Hoa am anh voi dong tien. Lai tren giay khong quan trong bang tien that trong tui.

Giai thich:

Dong tien la su chenh lech giua tien thu vao va tien chi ra trong mot khoang thoi gian nhat dinh.

Cong thuc: Dong tien = Tien thu vao - Tien chi ra

PHEP TINH TOI UU LOI NHUAN

3. Ty le Lai gop (Gross Profit Margin)

Ket Qua Ty le Lai gop

Ty le lai gop: 0%

Neu chi so nay qua thap, hoac la gia ban cua ban qua re, hoac la gia nhap nguyen lieu cua ban qua cao.

Giai thich tu ngu:

Ty le Lai gop cho biet phan tram loi nhuan con lai sau khi tru gia von hang ban.

Cong thuc: Lai gop (%) = ((Doanh thu - Gia von hang ban) / Doanh thu) x 100

Gia von hang ban (COGS) la chi phi truc tiep de san xuat/san xuat hang hoa ban ra.

PHEP TINH QUAN TRI

6. Doanh thu tren moi met vuong (Revenue per Square Meter)

Ket Qua Doanh thu/m²

Doanh thu tren moi met vuong: 0 dong/m²

Giup ban biet lieu mat bang co dang duoc su dung hieu qua hay khong.

Giai thich tu ngu:

Doanh thu tren moi met vuong la chi so do luong hieu qua su dung mat bang kinh doanh.

Cong thuc: Doanh thu/m² = Tong doanh thu / Tong dien tich quan

PHEP TINH VE KHACH HANG

8. Gia tri don hang Trung binh (Average Order Value - AOV)

Ket Qua AOV

Gia tri don hang trung binh: 0 dong

Tim moi cach de tang chi so nay (upsell, cross-sell, combo...).

Giai thich tu ngu:

Gia tri don hang Trung binh (AOV) la so tien trung binh moi khach hang chi tieu trong mot lan mua hang.

Cong thuc: AOV = Tong doanh thu / Tong so hoa don

Upsell: Ban phien ban cao cap hon cua san pham khach dang mua.

Cross-sell: Ban san pham bo sung lien quan den san pham khach dang mua.

Bai hoc cot loi: Kinh doanh khong phai la cam tinh. dang sau tu duy "lam chu" cua nguoi Hoa la su am anh voi nhung con so.

Ai nam duoc nhung con so nay, nguoi do nam duoc van menh cua doanh nghiep minh.
