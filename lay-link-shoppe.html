Tool lay san pham Shopee → CSV

Ho tro 3 che do: Shop (lay tat ca san pham trong 1 shop), Tu khoa (lay ket qua tim kiem), Flash Sale / Category (lay theo link danh muc/flash sale).
Chay tren dien thoai/PC. Neu Shopee chan CORS, tool se cung cap phuong an du phong (dan danh sach link).
Ket qua: 0 san pham
Goi y dinh dang CSV (dau vao tu tool thu 2):
name,link,price,commission_percent,sold,shop_name,rating

Luu y ky thuat: Shopee co co che han che (CORS, rate-limit). Tool co gang goi cac API khong chinh thuc cua Shopee — co the hoat dong ngay tren trinh duyet, nhung neu trinh duyet chan, ban co the chuyen sang che do dan link (nhanh va chac chan).
