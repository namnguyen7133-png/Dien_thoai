PHAN II: THUC dON THAY THE 7 NGAY

Moi ngay thiet ke cho mot the trang thuong gap, day du dinh luong, tinh am duong va calo.

🗓 NGAY 1 – THE PHI, KHAT, PHU (Thua can, khat nuoc, co phu nhe)

🌅 BUA SANG (6h30-7h30): Chao Yen Mach dau den
Nguyen lieuYen mach can mong: 40g kho. dau den: 20g (ngam mem). Nuoc: 400ml.
Am DuongTrung (Yen mach) Am (dau den). Giup thanh nhiet, loi thuy.
Calo~260 kcal
Cong Hieu / Ung Dung Chat xo hoa tan trong yen mach (Beta-glucan) giup giam hap thu cholesterol va duong. dau den ho tro chuc nang than, loi tieu nhe, giam phu. La bua sang ly tuong cho nguoi tieu duong type 2 kem mo mau.
🍱 BUA TRUA (11h30-12h30): Canh Ca Loc Gung
Nguyen lieuCa loc phi le: 120g. Gung tuoi: 3g (2 lat mong). Rau thi la: it la.
Am DuongTrung (Ca). Gung tinh am giup can bang, de tieu.
Calo~240 kcal
Cong Hieu / Ung Dung Ca loc giau dam de hap thu, it mo, giup no lau khong tang can. Gung lam am ty vi, chong day bung. Mon canh nay thay the hoan hao cho cac mon “bo am” nang ne nhu ba ba ham.
🌙 BUA TOI (18h-19h): Rau Luoc & dau Hu
Nguyen lieudau hu trang: 120g (1/2 bia). Rau cai xanh/bo xoi: 150g. Nuoc tuong nhat (dung it).
Am DuongAm. Mon an mat, nhe bung.
Calo~160 kcal
Cong Hieu / Ung Dung Giam tai cho he tieu hoa va tuyen tuy vao buoi toi. dam thuc vat tu dau hu khong gay ganh nang cho than. Chat xo tu rau giup lam cham hap thu duong.

📊 Tong nang luong ca ngay: ~660 kcal (Muc kiem soat calo tot cho nguoi can giam can).

🗓 NGAY 2 – PHOI YEU, KHO KHE (Hay ho, de cam, hut hoi)

🌅 Sang: Chao Gao Lut Ca Rot (Gao lut 40g, Ca rot 100g). Am nhe. ~230 kcal. Ca rot giau vitamin A tot cho niem mac ho hap.

🍱 Trua: Trung Ga Hap + Canh Rong Bien (Trung ga 1 qua, rong bien kho 5g). Am. ~180 kcal. Long trang trung bo phe am. Rong bien lam sach dom.

🌙 Toi: Ca Hap Bi dao (Ca diec/phi le 100g, Bi dao 150g). Am. ~220 kcal. Ca va bi dao ket hop giup thanh phe, tieu dom.

📊 Tong nang luong: ~630 kcal.

🗓 NGAY 3 – HU HAN NHE (So lanh, tay chan lanh, tieu hoa kem)

🌅 Sang: Chao Khoai Lang (Khoai lang 100g nghien nhuyen voi nuoc). Am. ~90 kcal. Am bung, nhuan trang nhe.

🍱 Trua: Thit Nac Luoc + Rau Cu (Thit heo nac vai 80g, su su luoc 100g). Trung. ~220 kcal. Cung cap dam va nang luong on dinh, khong lam lanh bung.

🌙 Toi: Canh Nam Huong (Nam huong tuoi 100g, dau hu non 50g). Am. ~80 kcal. Nam huong bo ty vi, tang cuong tieu hoa ma khong gay lanh.

📊 Tong nang luong: ~390 kcal (Ngay nhe, giam tai cho ty vi).

Ghi chu vong lap: Ngay 4-7 se luan phien ap dung nguyen tac va nguyen lieu cua cac ngay tren, vi du: Ngay 4 nhu Ngay 1 nhung doi ca thanh uc ga, Ngay 5 nhu Ngay 2 doi canh rong bien thanh canh bi dao… dam bao da dang va khong nham chan.

PHAN IV: KIENG KY VOI THUOC TAY

💊 KHI DUNG METFORMIN / INSULIN:
Tranh dung cung ngay: Kho qua (muop dang) nau qua dac, Tra la sen dam dac, Nuoc dau den/dau xanh nguyen chat.
→ Ly do: Ca thuoc va cac thuc pham nay deu co tac dung ha duong huyet. Dung chung de dan den tut duong huyet dot ngot (ha duong huyet), gay chong mat, va mo hoi, nguy hiem.

💊 KHI DUNG STATIN (Atorvastatin, Rosuvastatin...):
Tuyet doi tranh: Buoi chum (grapefruit), nuoc ep buoi. Han che toi da Ruou.
→ Ly do: Buoi lam tang nong do thuoc trong mau, tang nguy co ton thuong gan va tieu co van. Ruou cung lam tang ganh nang cho gan.

💊 KHI DUNG THUOC HA HUYET AP:
Khong tu y uong lien tuc: Cac loai tra loi tieu manh (tra ma de, rau ngo, bong ma de) hoac an che do qua nhat.
→ Ly do: Co the gay mat can bang dien giai (thieu kali), dan den met moi, tham chi lam loan nhip tim. Luon uong du nuoc loc.
