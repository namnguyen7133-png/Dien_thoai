Thanh Phan Goc Ly Do Xu Ly Muc do

Ruou trang, ruou thuoc Lam ton thuong gan, tuong tac nguy hiem voi hau het thuoc (Metformin gay nhiem toan, Statin hai gan). Gay ha/tang duong huyet that thuong. ❌ LOAI BO
