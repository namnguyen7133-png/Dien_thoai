Chuyen hoa vi thuoc kho tim thanh thuc pham pho thong • Co am duong • Calo • Gia tien

📘 I. NGUYEN TAC CHUNG (DUNG AN TOAN)

⚠ QUAN TRONG: Thuc pham chi ho tro, khong thay the toa thuoc bac si. Luon tham van bac si truoc khi thay doi che do an.

1⃣ Cach Thay The Theo dong – Tay Y

Vi Thuoc Bac Kho Tim Thay Bang (Cho Binh Dan) Tinh Chat Tuong dong
Hoang ky dau den, Khoai lang Bo khi Kien ty
dang sam Khoai mo, Yen mach Duong am Bo ty vi
Y di (hat bo bo) Bo bo kho, Bap Tru thap Loi tieu
Ky tu Tao do, Nho kho Bo can than Mat gan

2⃣ Phan Loai Am – Duong (Rat Quan Trong)

DUONG HU Trieu chung: Lanh nguoi, dau lung, met moi, so lanh
Dung mon: Am nong, bo duong (gung, thit bo, ga)
AM HU Trieu chung: Nong trong, kho mieng, mat ngu, but rut
Dung mon: Mat, bo am (dau den, hat sen, bi dao)
THAP – PHONG Trieu chung: dau khop, nang nguoi, phu nhe
Dung mon: Loi tieu, tru thap (bo bo, bap, dau xanh)

🩺 II. TRA THEO BENH (Kem Thuc don Thay The)

🦴 1. dAU LUNG – THAN YEU – NHUC MOI GOI

🥣 Chao dau den – Bo Bo – Gung
~320 kcal
Nguyen lieu: dau den (50g) • Bo bo (30g) • Gao te (40g) • Gung (5g)
Am Duong: Trung tinh → Hoi duong
Cong dung: Bo than • Giam dau lung • Loi tieu • Thay the cac bai "bo than trang duong" phuc tap
🐟 Ca Chep Nau dau do
~420 kcal
Nguyen lieu: Ca chep (400g) • dau do (50g) • Gung, hanh
Am Duong: Duong
Cong dung: Than duong hu • Tieu tien yeu • Phu nhe
🚫 Kieng ky khi dung thuoc Tay:
  • Thuoc giam dau/khang viem: ❌ Ruou • Ot • Ca phe
  • Thuoc huyet ap: ❌ Nuoc dua • Cam thao

😴 2. MAT NGU – HOI HOP – LO AU

🍚 Chao Hat Sen – Yen Mach
~280 kcal
Nguyen lieu: Hat sen (40g) • Yen mach (30g) • Gao (30g)
Am Duong: Am
Cong dung: An than • Ngu sau • Giam hoi hop • Thay cho "an than tru phien"
🚫 Kieng ky:
  • ❌ Tra dac • Ca phe sau 3 gio chieu
  • ❌ Tuyet doi khong: Uong thuoc ngu + Ruou
  • ❌ An qua no truoc khi ngu

🚽 3. TIEU TIEN KHONG dEU – PHU – THAP

🍵 Canh Bap – dau den
~230 kcal
Nguyen lieu: Bap (1 trai) • dau den (50g)
Am Duong: Trung tinh
Cong dung: Loi tieu nhe • Giam phu • Thap nhiet
🚫 Kieng ky khi dung thuoc Tay:
  • Thuoc loi tieu: ❌ An man nhieu • Nuoc ngot co gas
  • ❌ Tu y uong them tra loi tieu dam
