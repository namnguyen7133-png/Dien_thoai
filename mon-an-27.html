LUU Y QUAN TRONG

Thong tin nay chi mang tinh chat ho tro, tham khao va dua tren nguyen ly dong y. Khong thay the chan doan, tu van hay phac do dieu tri cua bac si. Can tham khao y kien chuyen gia truoc khi ap dung, dac biet neu dang dung thuoc Tay hoac co benh nen.

II. TRA CUU THEO BENH: THUC dON & KIENG KY

A. CAO HUYET AP – TIM MACH

Nguyen tac: An nhat, nhieu chat xo, kali, giam mo.

Chao gao lut - rau bap - nam meo
Am ~260 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Gao lut (80g), rau bap (50g), nam tai meo (10g)

Cong dung: Ha huyet ap, loi tieu, giam mo mau

Canh ga - bi dao - can tay
Am ~380 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Ga ta (300g), bi dao (200g), can tay (50g)

Cong dung: Thanh nhiet, ha ap, bo khi

KIENG KY khi dung thuoc huyet ap:
  • Cam thao (lam tang huyet ap)
  • Ruou bia, chat kich thich
  • An man, thuc pham che bien san
  • Noi tang dong vat

B. dAU NHUC XUONG KHOP – PHONG THAP

Nguyen tac: Giu am, giam thuc pham sinh dam thap.

Chao thit bo - gung
Duong ~420 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Gao te (70g), thit bo (150g), gung (10g)

Cong dung: Am khop, giam dau, bo khi huyet

Canh xuong heo - dau den
Duong ~450 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Xuong ong (500g), dau den (70g)

Cong dung: Bo can than, manh gan cot

KIENG KY:
  • Hai san song/lanh (tom, cua, ghe)
  • Mang, ca muoi
  • Ruou (dac biet khi dung thuoc giam dau)
  • do nep, duong tinh luyen

C. SUY NHUOC - MAT NGU - THIEU MAU

Nguyen tac: Bo de tieu, uu tien thuc pham bo mau, an than.

Chao trung ga - hat sen
Am ~310 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Gao (60g), trung ga (1 qua), hat sen (30g)

Cong dung: Duong tam, an than, bo khi huyet

Canh gan heo - bi do
Am ~290 kcal

Nguyen lieu: Gan heo (100g), bi do (200g)

Cong dung: Bo huyet, duong can, sang mat

KIENG KY:
  • Tra dac, ca phe (voi mat ngu)
  • do an song lanh
  • Thuoc ngu + ruou (nguy hiem)
  • Khong uong sua dau nanh voi trung/gan
