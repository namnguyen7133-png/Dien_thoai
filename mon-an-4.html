🍵 HE THONG THUC dON & UNG DUNG dONG Y HIEN dAI

📋 THUC dON MAU 7 NGAY (du 6 Thanh Phan)

🗓 NGAY 1 - Khoi dau Nhe Nhang

🌅 Sang (6h30-7h30):
Chao Gao Lut Ca Rot Gao lut kho: 40g, Ca rot: 100g (bam nho) Trung Am nhe ~230 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung On dinh duong huyet buoi sang, cung cap chat xo hoa tan tu gao lut, vitamin A tu ca rot tot cho mat (nguoi tieu duong). De tieu hoa.
🍱 Trua (11h30-12h30):
Canh Ca Loc Bi dao Ca loc phi le: 120g, Bi dao: 200g, Gung: 2 lat Trung Am ~260 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung Ca loc cung cap protein chat luong cao, it mo, tot cho nguoi mo mau. Bi dao thanh nhiet, ho tro loi tieu nhe. Mon canh giup no lau, khong gay nang bung.
🌙 Toi (18h-19h):
dau Hu Hap Rau Cai dau hu non: 120g, Rau cai xanh: 150g, Nuoc tuong nhat Am ~160 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung dam thuc vat tu dau hu giup giam ganh nang cho than. Rau cai giau vitamin K, canxi. Mon an mat, nhe bung ve dem, phu hop nguoi co van de ve than nhe kem tieu duong.

📊 Tong ngay: ~650 kcal

🗓 NGAY 2 - Boi Bo Nhe

🌅 Sang:
Chao Yen Mach Me den (Thay the Chao Me co ruou) Yen mach can: 40g, Me den: 10g (rang thom) Trung Am ~260 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung Yen mach co Beta-glucan giup giam cholesterol xau. Me den (thay the Nhuc thung dung) co tac dung nhuan trang nhe, bo can than theo dong y, an toan hon va de tim.
🍱 Trua:
Nam Rom Xao Thit Nac Nam rom tuoi: 200g, Thit heo nac vai: 70g, Dau an: 5ml Am Duong nhe ~250 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung Nam rom giup tang cuong chat xo va vi chat, ho tro tieu hoa, giam cam giac day bung. Thit nac cung cap dam va kem. Phu hop nguoi tieu duong kem bung truong, tieu hoa kem.
🌙 Toi:
Canh Rong Bien Rong bien kho (wakame): 10g, Gung: 3g bam, dau hu non: 50g Am ~50 kcal
Cong Hieu / Tac Dung Rong bien giau i-ot tu nhien va alginate, ho tro giam hap thu chat beo, tot cho nguoi mo mau cao. Canh thanh mat, nhe bung cho bua toi.

📊 Tong ngay: ~560 kcal

🗓 NGAY 3 - Ngay Nhe Bung (Giam Tai)

Muc dich: Cho he tieu hoa va chuyen hoa nghi ngoi, phu hop khi duong huyet co xu huong cao hoac co the met moi.

🌅 Sang: Khoai lang luoc (100g) - Am - ~90 kcal. 🍱 Trua: Ca chep hap gung (120g) - Trung - ~240 kcal. 🌙 Toi: Canh rau ngot (200g) nau voi it nam - Am - ~60 kcal.

📊 Tong ngay: ~390 kcal

Ghi chu: Ngay 4-7 se lap vong va bien tau tu cac nguyen lieu chinh cua Ngay 1 & 2 (luan phien Ca, Ga nac, Trung, dau hu, cac loai rau xanh va canh khac nhau).

🏥 TRA CUU THEO BENH & THE TRANG

Tinh TrangMON NEN DUNG (Trong thuc don tren)MON CAN TRANH / HAN CHE
Tieu duong + Cao mo mau Canh bi dao, Canh rong bien, Ca cac loai hap/luoc, dau hu, Tra la sen (3-4 lan/tuan). Ruou, Chao dac gao trang, Thit mo, Da dong vat, Banh ngot, Nuoc ngot.
Tieu duong + Bung truong, tieu hoa kem Nam rom cac mon, Chao loang gao lut/yen mach, Gung (mot it trong nau an), Rau luoc chin ky. dau den nguyen hat dac, do an song/lanh, Thuc an chien xao nhieu dau, An qua no.
Tieu duong + Than hu nhe (tieu dem) Me den (it), Trung ga (1 qua/ngay), Ca loc/ca diec, Cai bo xoi. do "dai bo" nhu thit de, ruou thuoc, gia vi qua nong (ot, tieu nhieu), An man.

⚠ KIENG KY KHI dANG DUNG THUOC TAY

Voi Metformin / Insulin: Khong nen dung Kho qua (muop dang) dac, Tra la sen dam, Nuoc dau den nguyen chat trong cung ngay dung thuoc. De gay tut duong huyet qua muc.

Voi Thuoc Statin (giam mo mau): Tranh dung Buoi chum (grapefruit), Nuoc ep buoi va Ruou, vi co the lam tang tac dung phu len gan.

Voi Thuoc Ha Huyet Ap: Khong uong cac loai tra loi tieu manh (tra ma de, rau ngo) lien tuc ma khong hoi y kien bac si, tranh gay mat can bang dien giai.
