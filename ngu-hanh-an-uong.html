Ngu Hanh An Uong Theo Gio

Buoi sang – Vi chua nhe (Moc)

Muc dich: danh thuc gan, khoi dong tieu hoa

  • 1 coc nuoc am + 3–5 giot chanh
  • Hoac 1/2 qua cam

dinh luong: toi da 100–150g

5-7h

Buoi trua – Vi dang nhe (Hoa)

Muc dich: Xa nhiet, mat tim

  • 2–3 lat muop dang
  • 1 bat rau ma nho
11-13h

Buoi chieu – Vi ngot vua (Tho)

Muc dich: Nuoi ty vi, giu suc

  • Com, chao, khoai, bi do
  • 1 qua chuoi nho hoac tao
15-17h

Buoi toi – Vi cay nhe (Kim)

Muc dich: Thong khi, tranh day bung

  • 1–2 lat gung nau chin
  • Hanh, toi vua phai
19-21h

Ban dem – Vi man nhe (Thuy)

Muc dich: Giu tinh, bo than

  • 1 bat canh xuong nhat
21-23h

Tom tat nguyen tac: Sang chua – Trua dang – Chieu ngot – Toi cay – dem man

SUA CHUA TRONG NGU HANH

Huong dan an sua chua dung thoi diem theo Ngu Hanh

Sua chua thuoc hanh Tho (vi chua ngot, tinh mat, vao Ty Vi). Nhung tuy thoi diem an se tuong tac voi cac hanh khac.

TOT NHAT: Buoi chieu (15-17h)

Hanh Tho (Ty Vi)

  • Ty vi hoat dong manh nhat
  • Ho tro tieu hoa bua trua
  • Cung cap nang luong cuoi ngay
  • Loi khuan phat trien tot

Luong: 1 hop 100g

TOT: Sau bua sang 1-2h (9-10h)

Hanh Tho sinh Kim

  • Da day da tieu hoa xong
  • Bo sung loi khuan cho ruot
  • Chuan bi nang luong cho trua

Luong: 1/2 hop 50g

KHONG NEN: Buoi sang som (5-7h)

Hanh Moc (Gan)

  • Lam lanh ty vi khi moi ngu day
  • Uc che hoat dong cua gan
  • Gay day bung, kho tieu

TRANH: Buoi toi muon (sau 20h)

Hanh Thuy (Than)

  • Lam than phai lam viec nhieu
  • Tich tu dom, am trong co the
  • De gay ho, viem hong

MEO AN SUA CHUA THEO NGU HANH

Ket hop tot:

  • Voi chuoi (bo Tho)
  • Voi tao (bo Phe)
  • Voi mat ong (on Ty)

Tranh ket hop:

  • Voi do lanh (hai Ty)
  • Voi hai san (sinh dom)
  • Khi bung doi (ton Vi)
Nguyen tac vang: An sua chua o nhiet do phong (khong lanh), sau khi an no 30-60 phut, vao buoi chieu la tot nhat theo Ngu Hanh.
