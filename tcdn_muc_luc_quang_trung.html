🎮 TCDN CHUONG 1 – TRO CHOI TAI CHINH THUC TE

🔍 NHAP TINH HUONG TAI CHINH CUA BAN

Mo ta tinh hinh hien tai cua ban de nhan chien luoc phu hop:


📊 PHAN TICH TINH HUONG CUA BAN

📌 MENU NHANH - CHON TINH HUONG CUA BAN

📘 THUAT NGU TAI CHINH → dOI THUONG

Hieu dung de ra quyet dinh dung:

  • Von: Tien ban co the dung ma khong chet doi
  • Rui ro: Kha nang mat tien
  • Loi nhuan: Tien loi sau khi tru het chi phi
  • Dong tien: Tien vao – tien ra moi thang
  • Von vay: Tien muon → phai tra
  • Von chu: Tien cua minh → khong ap luc
  • Gia tri DN: Nguoi khac san sang mua DN ban gia bao nhieu
  • Tai san: Thu tao ra tien cho ban
  • No phai tra: Tien ban no nguoi khac
  • Thanh khoan: Kha nang chuyen thanh tien mat nhanh

Vi du thuc te:

Tinh huong: Anh A co 50 trieu, muon them 100 trieu de mo quan ca phe.

Phan tich: Von chu = 50 trieu, von vay = 100 trieu, tong von = 150 trieu. Rui ro cao vi neu kinh doanh that bai, anh A mat 50 trieu cua minh va van no 100 trieu.

🎯 TRANG THAI: VON = 0 / dANG NO / KHO KHAN TAI CHINH

QUYET dINH QUAN TRONG NHAT LUC NAY:

Khi khong co von, moi quyet dinh dau tu deu co rui ro cuc cao. Uu tien so 1 la tao dong tien va giam no.

Chien luoc IT RUI RO NHAT

  • ✔ Khong dau tu bang tien (chi dau tu bang thoi gian va cong suc)
  • ✔ dau tu kien thuc, ky nang co the kiem tien ngay
  • ✘ Tuyet doi khong vay de dau tu, choi chung khoan, coin
  • ✔ Tim viec lam ngay, bat ky viec gi co thu nhap

Vi du thuc te (1–10)

  • 1 di lam thue (giao do an, phuc vu, ban hang) de co dong tien ngay
  • 2 Hoc ky nang ban hang online (mien phi tren YouTube)
  • 3 Khong mo quan khi chua co tien, chua co kinh nghiem
  • 4 Khong choi coin, chung khoan, forex voi hy vong "doi doi"
  • 5 Ban suc lao dong truoc, tich luy sau
  • 6 Tich luy quy du phong (3-6 thang chi tieu co ban)
  • 7 Tra no lai cao truoc (vay tin dung, vay nong)
  • 8 Nhan them viec phu (lam online, part-time)
  • 9 Khong gop von lam an voi nguoi khac khi khong co tien
  • 10 Tap quan ly chi tieu bang ung dung (Money Lover, Misa)

Cau chuyen thuc te: Tu no nan den on dinh

Hoan canh: Chi B mat viec, no 30 trieu the tin dung, khong co tien tiet kiem.

Hanh dong: Chap nhan lam 2 cong viec: ban hang online buoi toi va phu quan ca phe ban ngay. Cat giam moi chi tieu khong can thiet. Tra het no the tin dung trong 8 thang.

Ket qua: Sau 1 nam, co 10 trieu tiet kiem va ky nang ban hang thanh thao.

📌 Bai hoc: Khi khong co von, ban thoi gian va suc lao dong la cach an toan nhat de tao dong tien.

👤 CA NHAN CO THU NHAP ON dINH

Lo trinh tai chinh an toan

  1. Giai doan 1: Co thu nhap on dinh, tien du moi thang
  2. Giai doan 2: Xay dung quy du phong (6-12 thang chi tieu)
  3. Giai doan 3: Hieu ro rui ro cua tung kenh dau tu
  4. Giai doan 4: Bat dau dau tu voi so tien nho de hoc hoi
  5. Giai doan 5: da dang hoa dau tu khi co kinh nghiem

Vi du thuc te (11–20)

  • 11 Gui tiet kiem ngan hang (ky han 6-12 thang) truoc khi dau tu
  • 12 Khong vay tieu dung mua dien thoai, xe may tra gop lai cao
  • 13 dau tu nho 1-2 trieu vao chung khoan de hoc, khong ky vong loi lon
  • 14 Khong all-in (don het tien) vao mot kenh dau tu duy nhat
  • 15 Chap nhan lai thap (6-8%/nam) nhung an toan thay vi lai cao rui ro lon
  • 16 Khong vay dau tu khi thu nhap bap benh hoac chua on dinh
  • 17 Chi dung tien nhan roi (sau khi tru chi tieu, du phong) de dau tu
  • 18 Chap nhan loi it, an toan thay vi tham loi cao
  • 19 Rut lui ngay khi thay dau hieu lo, khong co "go gac"
  • 20 Uu tien on dinh tai chinh ca nhan truoc khi nghi den lam giau nhanh

TINH HUONG QUYET dINH:

Ban co 50 trieu tien nhan roi, thu nhap 15 trieu/thang on dinh. Ban nen:

  • ✔ Gui 30 trieu tiet kiem (an toan, thanh khoan cao)
  • ✔ dau tu 10 trieu vao chung khoan (de hoc hoi)
  • ✔ dau tu 10 trieu vao ban than (hoc them ky nang)
  • ✘ dau tu het 50 trieu vao mot du an "hot" ban khong hieu ro

🏪 DOANH NGHIEP NHO / STARTUP

Nguyen tac song con

  • Dong tien la mach mau - phai quan ly chat che hang ngay
  • Khong mo rong quy mo khi chua co loi nhuan on dinh it nhat 6 thang
  • Vay it, vay ngan han, quay vong von nhanh
  • Uu tien khach hang tra tien truoc, tra tien ngay

Vi du thuc te (21–30)

  • 21 Mo quan nho, dia diem binh dan truoc khi thue mat bang dat tien
  • 22 Khong thue mat bang lon, dep khi chua co du khach hang on dinh
  • 23 Uu tien khach hang thanh toan ngay, giam gia cho khach tra tien mat
  • 24 Tranh ton kho lon, chi nhap hang khi co don dat truoc
  • 25 Khong vay dai han khi chua chac chan ve dong tien tuong lai
  • 26 Tai dau tu it nhat 50% loi nhuan vao phat trien kinh doanh
  • 27 Khong chia het tien loi, giu lai quy du phong cho doanh nghiep
  • 28 Cat lo som voi san pham/dich vu khong ban duoc sau 3 thang
  • 29 Khong mo them chi nhanh khi chua co he thong quan ly bai ban
  • 30 Uu tien song sot qua khung hoang thay vi tang truong nhanh

Kich ban kinh doanh thuc te:

Tinh huong: Quan ca phe nho co doanh thu 30 trieu/thang, loi nhuan 8 trieu/thang.

Quyet dinh sai: Vay 200 trieu mo chi nhanh moi tai vi tri dat do.

Rui ro: Chi nhanh moi can 50 khach/ngay moi hoa von, nhung chi dat 20 khach/ngay. Lo 15 trieu/thang, de doa ca quan goc.

Quyet dinh dung: Toi uu quan hien tai, tang loi nhuan len 10-12 trieu/thang. Sau 1-2 nam tich luy, mo chi nhanh nho hon voi von tu co.

🏢 DOANH NGHIEP LON / CO QUY MO

Chien luoc theo chu ky kinh te

  • Thi truong tang truong: Mo rong, dau tu, vay von voi lai suat co dinh
  • Thi truong on dinh: Cung co thi phan, toi uu chi phi
  • Thi truong suy thoai: Giu tien mat, mua lai doi thu gap kho khan
  • Khung hoang: Song con la uu tien, cat giam moi chi phi khong can thiet

Vi du thuc te (31–40)

  • 31 Phat hanh trai phieu doanh nghiep khi lai suat thap de co von dai han
  • 32 Dung la chan thue hop phap (dau tu vao nghien cuu, phat trien)
  • 33 da dang hoa dau tu vao nhieu nganh, giam rui ro tap trung
  • 34 Khong phu thuoc vao 1 thi truong, 1 nha cung cap, 1 khach hang lon
  • 35 Mua lai doanh nghiep cung nganh khi ho gap kho khan (gia thap)
  • 36 Giu ty le tien mat cao khi thay dau hieu khung hoang kinh te
  • 37 Khong vay qua 60% tong tai san, duy tri he so no an toan
  • 38 So sanh chi phi von vay va chi phi von chu truoc khi quyet dinh huy dong von
  • 39 danh gia rui ro quoc gia, rui ro nganh truoc khi dau tu lon
  • 40 Toi da hoa gia tri doanh nghiep trong dai han thay vi loi nhuan ngan han

Case study: Chien luoc cua doanh nghiep lon trong khung hoang

Tinh huong 2020: dai dich COVID-19, nhieu doanh nghiep dong cua, thieu tien mat.

Chien luoc doanh nghiep thong minh:

  1. Cat giam ngay 30% chi phi khong thiet yeu
  2. dam phan gia han no voi ngan hang
  3. Tan dung chinh sach ho tro cua Nha nuoc
  4. Chuyen doi mo hinh kinh doanh (offline → online)
  5. Mua lai doi thu yeu the voi gia bang 30-50% gia tri thuc

Ket qua: Khi kinh te phuc hoi, doanh nghiep nay co thi phan lon hon, manh hon truoc khung hoang.
