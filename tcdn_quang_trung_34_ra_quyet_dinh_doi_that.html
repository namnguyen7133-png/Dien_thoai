2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc (giai thich doi thuong)

PMT – So tien gui/nhan moi ky
👉 Ngoai doi: tien ban gui tiet kiem moi thang / moi nam

i – Lai suat moi ky
👉 Gia cua viec dung tien (nam / thang phai thong nhat)

n – So ky
👉 So nam, so thang ban cho

Chuoi tien deu cuoi ky
→ Gui tien sau khi het ky (cuoi nam, cuoi thang)

Cong thuc FV:
FV = PMT × [ (1 + i)n − 1 ] / i

👉 Ap dung: gui tiet kiem cuoi nam, dong hoc phi cuoi ky

Chuoi tien deu dau ky
→ Gui tien ngay dau ky

Cach tinh:
FV dau ky = FV cuoi ky × (1 + i)

👉 Ap dung: tra tien thue nha dau thang, gop von ngay

Chuoi tien khong deu
→ Moi ky tien khac nhau

Cach lam:
✔ Quy tung khoan ve hien tai (PV) hoac tuong lai (FV) ✔ Sau do cong tong

👉 Ap dung: kinh doanh, dau tu, thu nhap freelancer

3.2 Khi nao lam? Lam gi truoc – sau? (Tro choi kinh doanh thuc te)

TRINH TU CHUAN NGOAI dOI (Chien luoc it rui ro):
  1. Xac dinh loai chuoi tien (deu / khong deu) trong tinh huong kinh doanh.
  2. Chon lai suat thi truong thuc te (hien nay ~5-7%/nam tiet kiem VN 2025).
  3. Tinh PV → xem du an co dang dau tu khong (NPV >0).
  4. Tinh FV → du bao loi nhuan dai han.
  5. Uoc luong rui ro thua lo toi da (khong qua 30% von).
  6. Ap dung da dang hoa: ket hop chuoi deu (an toan) va khong deu (tang truong).
  7. Theo doi thoi diem: uu tien dau ky neu co loi the lai kep som.
CHIEN LUOC KINH DOANH THUC TE IT RUI RO:
  • Doanh nghiep nho: uu tien dong tien deu (subscription model nhu Netflix).
  • Tap doan: dung chuoi khong deu cho tang truong, nhung hedge rui ro.
  • Luon so sanh voi lai ngan hang: chi lam neu ky vong > lai tiet kiem + rui ro.
  • Ap dung dollar-cost averaging de giam bien dong.

🎮 Tro choi thuc te: Hay tuong tuong ban la CEO startup. Chon tinh huong duoi, tinh toan va quyet dinh de toi uu loi nhuan, giam rui ro!
