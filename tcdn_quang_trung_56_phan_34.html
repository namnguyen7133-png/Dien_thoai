📘 Thuat ngu tai chinh (Giai thich de hieu)

  • PV (Present Value): Gia tri tien ngay hom nay
  • FV (Future Value): Gia tri tien trong tuong lai
  • i (Lai suat): Chi phi su dung tien theo thoi gian
  • n (So ky): So nam/thang vay hoac dau tu
  • EAR (Effective Annual Rate): Lai suat thuc te sau khi ghep lai
💡 10 trieu hom nay > 10 trieu sau 5 nam vi lam phat va co hoi dau tu.

📐 Cong thuc quan trong nhat

Gia tri tuong lai (FV): FV = PV × (1 + i)n
Gia tri hien tai (PV): PV = FV / (1 + i)n
Lai suat thuc (i): i = (FV / PV)1/n − 1
👉 Dung de quyet dinh: "Lay tien ngay hay cho?"

🔍 AP DUNG THUC TE HOAN CHINH - LO TRINH 7 BUOC

📋 Buoc 1: danh gia hien trang tai chinh

Thuc hanh: Liet ke toan bo tai san (tien mat, tiet kiem, dau tu, bat dong san) va no (vay ngan hang, the tin dung, no ca nhan).
Cong cu: Su dung cong thuc: Gia tri rong = Tong tai san - Tong no

🎯 Buoc 2: Xac dinh muc tieu tai chinh theo thoi gian

Ngan han (0-1 nam): Tra het no the tin dung, xay dung quy khan cap 3-6 thang chi phi sinh hoat.
Trung han (1-5 nam): Tiet kiem mua xe, dat coc mua nha, du lich gia dinh.
Dai han (5-20 nam): Mua nha, giao duc con cai, nghi huu som.

💰 Buoc 3: Lap ngan sach va ke hoach tiet kiem

Quy tac 50-30-20: 50% cho nhu cau thiet yeu, 30% cho mong muon ca nhan, 20% cho tiet kiem & dau tu.
Tu dong hoa: Thiet lap chuyen khoan tu dong vao tai khoan tiet kiem ngay khi nhan luong.

📈 Buoc 4: Xay dung danh muc dau tu da dang

Nguyen tac: da dang hoa theo tuoi: (100 - tuoi)% vao co phieu/tai san rui ro, phan con lai vao trai phieu/tai san an toan.
Vi du: Nam 30 tuoi → 70% co phieu/quy ETF, 30% trai phieu/tien gui.

🛡 Buoc 5: Quan ly va phong ngua rui ro

Bao hiem co ban: Y te, nhan tho (neu co nguoi phu thuoc), tai nan, bao hiem nha/tai san.
Quy khan cap: Luon duy tri 3-6 thang chi phi sinh hoat trong tai khoan de tiep can.

🧾 Buoc 6: Toi uu hoa thue

dau tu uu dai thue: Quy huu tri tu nguyen, bao hiem nhan tho, trai phieu chinh phu.
Khai thue thu nhap: Tan dung cac khoan giam tru gia canh, dong gop tu thien.

🔄 Buoc 7: danh gia va dieu chinh dinh ky

Hang quy: Xem xet danh muc dau tu, dieu chinh phan bo neu can.
Hang nam: danh gia lai muc tieu, dieu chinh ke hoach theo thay doi cuoc song (ket hon, sinh con, thay doi cong viec).

Giai phap: Can tiet kiem them 40 trieu/thang voi lai suat dau tu 8%/nam de dat muc tieu.
Tinh toan: FV = 200tr*(1+0.08)^5 + [40tr*(((1+0.08)^5 - 1)/0.08)] = 294tr + 235tr*12 = ~3 ty

🎮 Bang dieu Khien Tinh Huong Thuc Te

🎯 Muc tieu tro choi: Ban la nha quyet dinh tai chinh o nhieu cap do. Chon phuong an vay/thue/mua co PV chi phi thap nhat va rui ro it nhat de thang cuoc!

📘 Thuat ngu trong tam

  • Amortization: Bang phan bo tra no goc + lai tung ky
  • PMT: Khoan thanh toan co dinh moi ky
  • PV (Present Value): Gia tri hien tai cua dong tien tuong lai
  • Du no giam dan: Goc con lai sau moi ky tra
  • Chiet khau dong tien: Quy tat ca ve gia tri hom nay de so sanh cong bang
💡 Quy tac vang: Luon chon phuong an co PV nho nhat → Chi phi thuc te re nhat!

📐 Cong thuc cot loi

Thanh toan deu PMT:
PMT = PV × (i × (1+i)^n) / ((1+i)^n - 1)
Gia tri hien tai PV cua dong tien:
PV = Σ (CF_t / (1+i)^t)
👉 Trong kinh doanh thuc te: Chon lai suat chiet khau = chi phi von (WACC) hoac lai vay ngan hang

👤 25 Tinh Huong Thuc Te Cap do Ca Nhan

🎲 Thu thach: Ban la nguoi tre doc lap, chon cach tai chinh thong minh nhat de tranh bay lai suat.
  1. Vay ngan hang mua nha tra gop 30 nam vs thue nha dai han: Tinh PV tong chi phi → thuong mua nha PV thap hon neu lai co dinh.
  2. Mua xe o to tra gop 5 nam vs tra thang: So PV, neu lai cao → tra thang tot hon.
  3. Vay tieu dung mua dien thoai/iPhone tra gop 0% lai: Thuc te co phi an → tinh PV thuc.
  4. Vay tin chap tieu dung vs vay the chap: Lai tin chap cao → PV dat hon nhieu.
  5. Mua bao hiem nhan tho tich luy vs gui tiet kiem: So PV gia tri hoan lai.
  6. Tra gop the tin dung vs tra day du: Lai the cao → tranh tra toi thieu.
  7. Vay hoc phi du hoc vs tu tich luy: Tinh PV chi phi lai vay.
  8. Mua laptop tra gop vs cho tich tien: Neu lai thap → tra gop giu tien dau tu khac.
  9. dau tu chung khoan vay margin vs dung tien tu co: Rui ro cao neu thi truong giam.
  10. Thue tai chinh mua xe may vs vay ngan hang: So PV tong thanh toan.
  11. Mua do noi that tra gop vs mua cu: Tinh chi phi co hoi.
  12. Vay nong ngoai luong vs ngan hang: Lai cat co → PV cuc cao.
  13. Tham gia quy tin dung den vs tiet kiem ca nhan: Rui ro mat trang.
  14. Mua vang tra gop vs tich luy dan: Neu gia vang tang manh → tra gop loi.
  15. Vay de dau tu bat dong san luot song: Rui ro lai suat tang.
  16. Su dung vi dien tu tra sau (like Fundiin): Phi an → tinh PV.
  17. Mua ve may bay tra gop vs tra ngay: Thuong 0% that → loi.
  18. Vay de hoc them ky nang (khoa hoc online): Neu tang thu nhap → PV loi nhuan duong.
  19. Tra no the tin dung som vs dau tu khac: Lai the cao → tra som uu tien.
  20. Mua qua cuoi tra gop vs tiet kiem truoc: Ap luc tai chinh ca nhan.
  21. Vay de du lich vs tiet kiem: Thuong khong dang PV.
  22. Su dung BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) mua sam: De roi bay chi tieu qua tay.
  23. Vay peer-to-peer (P2P lending): Lai cao hon ngan hang.
  24. dau tu crypto vay don bay: Rui ro thanh ly cuc cao.
  25. Mua hop dong tuong lai vs tien mat: Ca nhan thuong thua lo.
