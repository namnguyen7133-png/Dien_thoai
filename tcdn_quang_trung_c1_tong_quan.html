🎬 Video 1 – Tong quan Tai chinh Doanh nghiep

📌 Ba triet ly song con trong Tai chinh Doanh nghiep

① Co lai chua chac an toan

Ban chat: Lai ke toan = An toan tai chinh.

  • Doanh nghiep lai nhung khong thu duoc tien → van pha san
  • Doanh nghiep tu nhan lai cao nhung chu phai chiu no bang tai san ca nhan
📍 Vi du doi thuong: Ban ban hang loi 200 trieu nhung khach chua tra tien → den han tra no ngan hang → van sap.

② Dong tien quan trong hon loi nhuan

Ban chat: Tien that quyet dinh su song con.

  • Lai chi la con so tren giay
  • Dong tien moi la tien tra duoc luong – no – chi phi
📍 Vi du: Vay von co lai vay → loi nhuan giam → nhung it thue hon → tien mat giu lai nhieu hon.

③ Tien hom nay > tien ngay mai

Ban chat: Tien co kha nang sinh loi theo thoi gian.

  • 1 trieu hom nay co the thanh 1.1 trieu nam sau
  • Giu tien chet la mat co hoi
📍 Vi du: Nhan 100 trieu hom nay tot hon 120 trieu sau 5 nam neu khong dau tu.

🧠 Ung dung thuc te

  • Khoi nghiep von 0: song bang dong tien, khong song bang lai
  • Nghe an danh: phai tach tien sinh hoat va tien lam an
  • Thu nhap cao: phai bien tien thanh tai san sinh loi

🎯 Muc tieu: Hieu – Nho – Ap dung duoc vao doi song & cong viec

I. Ba triet ly tai chinh cot loi

❓ Cau hoi 1: Vi sao “Co lai chua chac an toan”?

✅ Tra loi: Vi loi nhuan chi la con so ke toan, con an toan tai chinh phu thuoc vao dong tien va kha nang tra no dung han.

  • Doanh nghiep tu nhan lai cao nhung chu phai chiu trach nhiem vo han
  • Lai ma khong thu duoc tien → van pha san
❓ Cau hoi 2: The nao la mat an toan thanh khoan?

✅ Tra loi: La khi doanh nghiep khong co tien mat de tra luong, tra no dung han du tren so sach van co lai.

📍 Vi du: Khach no tien – ngan hang toi han → doanh nghiep sap.
❓ Cau hoi 3: Vi sao dong tien quan trong hon loi nhuan?

✅ Tra loi: Vi dong tien la tien that dung de chi tra, con loi nhuan chi la ket qua tinh toan.

  • Dong tien quyet dinh song – chet
  • Loi nhuan khong tra duoc no
❓ Cau hoi 4: La chan thue la gi?

✅ Tra loi: La loi ich thue khi dung von vay, vi lai vay duoc tru truoc thue → giam so thue phai nop.

📍 Ket luan: Vay hop ly → loi nhuan ke toan thap hon → nhung dong tien giu lai cao hon.
❓ Cau hoi 5: “Tien hom nay > tien ngay mai” nghia la gi?

✅ Tra loi: Vi tien hom nay co the dem di dau tu sinh loi, con tien ngay mai thi khong.

  • Lien quan den chi phi co hoi
  • La nen tang dinh gia du an

II. Ung dung trong cac truong hop dac thu

❓ Khoi nghiep von 0 dong can chu y dieu gi?

✅ Tra loi:

  • Dong tien la su song
  • Thu tien truoc – tra tien sau
  • Khong nhin lai khi tien chua ve
❓ Nghe an danh ap dung TCDN the nao?

✅ Tra loi:

  • Tach tien sinh hoat & tien dau tu
  • Quan tri rui ro ca nhan
  • Hieu thue de giu tien that
❓ Nghe thu nhap cao nhung khong ben thi sao?

✅ Tra loi:

  • Tai dau tu som khi tien con gia tri cao
  • Bien thu nhap thanh tai san
  • Khong tieu het loi nhuan

III. Kien thuc nen bat buoc nho

❓ Cac loai hinh doanh nghiep khac nhau the nao?

✅ Tra loi: Khac nhau o trach nhiem tai san va kha nang huy dong von.

(Xem bang so sanh chi tiet o phan tiep theo)

❓ Muc tieu cuoi cung cua quan tri tai chinh la gi?

✅ Tra loi: Toi da hoa gia tri tai san cho chu so huu, khong phai chi toi da hoa loi nhuan ngan han.

🎬 Video 2 – Gia tri thoi gian cua tien (Time Value of Money)

📌 Nguyen ly cot loi: 1 dong hom nay > 1 dong ngay mai

I. Khai niem nen tang

❓ Cau hoi 1: Chuoi tien te la gi?

✅ Tra loi: Chuoi tien te la tap hop cac khoan tien phat sinh theo thoi gian, co the deu hoac khong deu.

❓ Cau hoi 2: Phan biet tien dAU KY va CUOI KY?
  • Cuoi ky: Tien phat sinh sau khi ket thuc mot ky
  • dau ky: Tien phat sinh ngay dau ky → duoc huong them 1 ky lai
📍 Meo thi: Neu la dau ky → ket qua nhan them (1 + r)

II. Lai don va Lai kep

❓ Cau hoi 3: Lai don la gi?

✅ Tra loi: Lai chi tinh tren von goc ban dau.

Cong thuc:

FV = PV × (1 + r × n)

  • FV: Gia tri tuong lai
  • PV: Gia tri hien tai (von goc)
  • r: Lai suat moi ky
  • n: So ky
❓ Cau hoi 4: Lai kep la gi?

✅ Tra loi: Lai nhap goc, ky sau tinh lai tren ca goc + lai.

Cong thuc:

FV = PV × (1 + r)n

📍 Thuc te: Gui tiet kiem dai han, dau tu → luon dung lai kep

III. Gia tri tuong lai cua tien (Future Value – FV)

❓ Cau hoi 5: FV dung de lam gi?

✅ Tra loi: Dung de biet tuong lai co bao nhieu tien neu dau tu hom nay.

FV cua chuoi tien te deu – CUOI KY

Cong thuc:

FV = PMT × [ (1+r)n − 1 ] / r

  • PMT: So tien gui moi ky
  • r: Lai suat moi ky
  • n: So ky gui
FV cua chuoi tien te deu – dAU KY

Cong thuc:

FV = PMT × [ (1+r)n − 1 ] / r × (1+r)

📍 Chi khac CUOI KY o: × (1+r)

IV. Gia tri hien tai cua tien (Present Value – PV)

❓ Cau hoi 6: PV dung khi nao?

✅ Tra loi: Dung khi muon biet tien tuong lai hom nay dang gia bao nhieu.

PV cua mot khoan tien don

Cong thuc:

PV = FV / (1+r)n

  • FV: Tien nhan trong tuong lai
  • PV: Gia tri hom nay
PV cua chuoi tien te deu

Cong thuc:

PV = PMT × [1 − 1/(1+r)n] / r

V. Luu y cuc ky quan trong khi lam bai

  • ⚠ Phai thong nhat don vi r va n
  • ⚠ Lai nam → n tinh theo nam
  • ⚠ Lai thang → n tinh theo thang
📌 Ung dung thuc te:
  • Gui tiet kiem
  • Mua nha tra gop
  • Lap ke hoach tra no
  • So sanh du an dau tu
