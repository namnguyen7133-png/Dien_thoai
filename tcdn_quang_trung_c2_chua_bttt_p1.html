2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (hieu bang doi song)

Chuoi thoi gian & Chuoi tien te

Chuoi thoi gian: Truc nam/thang (0,1,2,3…)

Chuoi tien te: So tien phat sinh tai moi thoi diem

Vi du: Ban di lam 5 nam → la chuoi thoi gian Luong moi nam → la chuoi tien te

Lai don & Lai kep

  • Lai don: Lai chi tinh tren von goc
  • Lai kep: Lai sinh ra tiep tuc de lai

doi song: Gui tiet kiem lau dai → giau nho lai kep

FV – Gia tri tuong lai

FV cho biet tien hom nay se thanh bao nhieu trong tuong lai

Cong thuc: FV = PV × (1+r)n

PV – Gia tri hien tai

Quy doi tien tuong lai ve hien tai

Cong thuc: PV = FV / (1+r)n

⚠ Tien hom nay luon gia tri hon tien ngay mai

2.2 Dung trong cong viec hay ca nhan?

doi tuong Dung kien thuc nao Muc dich
Ca nhan Lai kep, FV Tiet kiem, tu do tai chinh
Nguoi di lam PV Khong bi lo khi tra gop
Doanh nghiep PV, dong tien Tranh dau tu sai
✔ Ca nhan: nhin tuong lai ✔ Doanh nghiep: nhin hien tai

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (theo tung thoi diem)

Buoc 1: Xac dinh von

Bo ra bao nhieu tien that?

Buoc 2: Xac dinh dong tien

Tien ve khi nao? deu hay khong deu?

Buoc 3: Tinh PV

Neu PV < von → khong nen lam

Buoc 4: Xet dieu kien thi truong

  • Lai suat cao → chiet khau manh
  • Rui ro cao → can PV lon hon von

Lai – Lo nhin the nao?

  • Lai ke toan = tien that
  • Khong co tien dung luc → pha san
