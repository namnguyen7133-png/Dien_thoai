2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (giai thich doi song)

PV – Present Value (Gia tri hien tai)

PV la so tien tuong duong o hien tai cua mot khoan tien trong tuong lai.

doi song: 100 trieu sau 5 nam khong bang 100 trieu hom nay.

Cong thuc: PV = FV / (1+r)n

PMT – Payment (Tien tra/gui moi ky)

So tien co dinh phai tra hoac gui deu moi ky.

di lam: tien tra gop nha moi thang.

NPER – Number of Periods

So ky can de dat muc tieu tai chinh.

Vi du: Bao lau thi tiet kiem du tien cuoi?

RATE – Lai suat thuc

Lai suat thuc te an trong khoan vay/tra gop.

Thuc te: Tra gop “0%” nhung tong tien cao hon.

✔ Quy tac song con: Tien chi ra = Am (-) | Tien thu ve = Duong (+)

3.2 Moi thoi diem nen lam gi? (theo tu duy tai chinh)

Buoc 1: Xac dinh dong tien

Moi thang tao ra bao nhieu tien that?

Buoc 2: Tinh PMT

PMT ≤ 40% thu nhap → an toan

Buoc 3: So sanh PV

Chon phuong an co PV thap nhat (chi phi nho nhat)

dieu kien thi truong

  • Lai suat tang → han che vay
  • Kinh doanh rui ro → can an han

Lai – lo nhin the nao?

  • Lai ke toan = tien mat
  • PMT khong tra noi → pha san
