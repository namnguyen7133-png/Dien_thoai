🔗 Quang Trung TV – Chua bai tap trong tam phan 3 (Lap ke hoach tra no)

2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & ap dung thuc te

Dong tien tra no (CF)

So tien phai tra co dinh moi ky (gom goc + lai).

doi song: tien tra gop mua nha moi nam/thang.

Du no goc

So tien con no ngan hang tai tung thoi diem.

di lam: biet luc nao ap luc tra no cao nhat.

An han goc

Giai doan chi tra lai, chua tra goc.

Thuc te: doanh nghiep moi vay von.

Cong thuc trong tam

CF = PV × r / [1 − (1+r)-n]

👉 Cong thuc nay giup:
  • Tinh tien tra moi nam
  • Lap bang goc – lai

2.2 Dung trong cong viec hay ca nhan?

  • Ca nhan: vay mua nha, xe
  • Nguoi di lam: danh gia kha nang tra no
  • Doanh nghiep: tranh pha san do thieu dong tien
❌ Chi nhin lai suat ✔ Phai lap bang tra no

3.2 Trinh tu lam dung (theo bai giang)

  1. Xac dinh so nam thuc su tra goc + lai
  2. Tinh CF
  3. Lap bang tung ky
  4. Kiem tra du no cuoi = 0

Khi nao nen vay?

  • Dong tien du tra CF
  • Loi nhuan > lai vay

Rui ro

  • CF cao → thieu tien van hanh
  • An han xong → soc dong tien
