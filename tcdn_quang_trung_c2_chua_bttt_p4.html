2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & ap dung doi song

PV – Gia tri hien tai

So tien quy doi ve thoi diem hom nay.

doi song: Gia tri that cua tra gop, vay mua nha.

Ca nhan Doanh nghiep

CF – Dong tien deu

So tien tra/thu giong nhau moi ky.

di lam: Tra no ngan hang hang nam.

Lai suat (r)

Chi phi su dung von hoac loi nhuan ky vong.

Cong thuc trong tam

PV cua dong tien deu:

PV = CF × [1 − (1+r)-n] / r

👉 Cong thuc nay dung de:

  • Tinh so tien phai tra moi nam
  • So sanh mua dut hay tra gop

2.2 Dung trong cong viec hay ca nhan?

  • Ca nhan: vay mua nha, mua xe, tra gop
  • Nguoi di lam: so sanh thue – mua tai san
  • Doanh nghiep: chon phuong an chi phi thap nhat (PV nho nhat)

❌ Khong dung tong tien cong gop ✔ Luon quy ve PV
