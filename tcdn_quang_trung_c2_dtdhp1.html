2.1 Thuat ngu & cong thuc kho (giai thich doi song)

FV – Future Value (Gia tri tuong lai)

FV tra loi: “Mon do hom nay se gia bao nhieu trong tuong lai?”

Cong thuc Excel: =FV(rate, nper, pmt, pv)

Vi du doi song:
  • Xe hom nay 1 ty, tang 5%/nam → 5 nam sau ≈ 1,276 ty
  • Khong tinh FV → tiet kiem mai van thieu tien

PMT – Payment (Tiet kiem / tra tien dinh ky)

PMT tra loi: “Moi nam/thang phai bo ra bao nhieu?”

❌ Sai lam pho bien: Tiet kiem 8%/nam nhung tai san tang 5%/nam → lai thuc rat thap

Type – Thoi diem dong tien

  • Type = 0: Gui cuoi ky (mac dinh)
  • Type = 1: Gui dau ky → it tien hon, an toan hon

2.2 Dung trong cong viec hay ca nhan?

doi tuong Dung ham Ly do
Ca nhan FV + PMT Khong bi thieu tien khi mua tai san
Gia dinh FV Hoc phi, nha dat luon tang gia
Startup PMT Chet vi dong tien chu khong phai vi lo
Doanh nghiep FV + PMT Thay may moc, mo rong san xuat
✔ Ca nhan: hoi “moi nam co du tien khong?” ✔ Doanh nghiep: hoi “tai san se doi gia bao nhieu?”

3.2 Trinh tu lam dung (theo bai giang)

Buoc 1: Tinh GIA THAT cua muc tieu

Dung FV de tinh gia tai san sau truot gia

Buoc 2: Xac dinh kha nang tich luy

Dung PMT de biet moi nam/thang can bao nhieu

Buoc 3: So voi thu nhap

  • PMT < 30% thu nhap → an toan
  • PMT 30–50% → rui ro
  • PMT > 50% → rat de vo ke hoach

Khi nao NEN thuc hien?

  • Thu nhap on dinh
  • Lai tiet kiem > lam phat tai san
  • Co quy du phong

Khi nao KHONG nen?

  • Thu nhap bap benh
  • Chi nhin lai ngan hang, quen truot gia
  • Phai vay de di tiet kiem
⚠ Lai danh nghia = lai thuc
