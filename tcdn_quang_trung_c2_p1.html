2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc tai chinh (Ham FV)

FV (Future Value) – Gia tri tuong lai cua tien.

Tien hom nay
Lai kep
Thoi gian

Cong thuc Excel:

=FV(rate, nper, pmt, pv, type)

  • rate: lai suat moi ky
  • nper: tong so ky
  • pmt: so tien gui them moi ky (am)
  • pv: von ban dau (am)
  • type: 0 (cuoi ky), 1 (dau ky)
💡 Hieu doi thuong:
– Ban co bao nhieu tien hom nay?
– Moi thang bo them bao nhieu?
– Sau X nam con lai bao nhieu?

3.2 Trinh tu ra quyet dinh tai chinh (Theo FV)

🔹 Buoc 1: Xac dinh von ban dau (pv)
🔹 Buoc 2: Xac dinh gui them moi ky (pmt)
🔹 Buoc 3: Kiem tra lai suat & lam phat
🔹 Buoc 4: Tinh FV
🔹 Buoc 5: So lai – rui ro – quyet dinh
⚠ Chi nen thuc hien khi:
– Lai > lam phat
– Co dong tien on dinh
– Khong anh huong sinh hoat

4.2 Nhap tinh huong thuc te

📌 Case: Gui tiet kiem

Dung FV de biet sau X nam co du tien mua nha khong.

📌 Case: Tich luy tu luong

Dung pmt deu dan de bien thu nhap nho thanh tai san lon.

📌 Case: Lo & rui ro

Neu FV < tong tien da bo → lo ngam vi lam phat.
