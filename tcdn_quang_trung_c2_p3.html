3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau?

① Xac dinh khoan vay (PV)
② Kiem tra dong tien tra duoc moi ky (PMT)
③ Tinh lai tung ky (IPMT)
④ Tinh thoi gian tra xong (NPER)
⑤ So voi thu nhap & rui ro
⑥ Quyet dinh vay / khong vay
Chi nen thuc hien khi:
– PMT ≤ 30–40% thu nhap
– IPMT giam dan theo thoi gian
– Thi truong & cong viec on dinh
Rui ro:
– IPMT cao keo dai → bao mon thu nhap
– NPER qua dai → ket tai chinh
