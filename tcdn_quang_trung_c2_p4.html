📘 TCDN1 – TU DUY & RA QUYET dINH TAI CHINH

Gia tri thoi gian cua tien – ap dung doi song & doanh nghiep

2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (hieu de dung)

🔹 PV – Present Value (Von hien tai)

So tien ban co hoac vay ngay luc nay.

doi song: Vay mua xe 200 trieu → PV = 200 trieu

🔹 FV – Future Value (Gia tri tuong lai)

So tien ban se co trong tuong lai sau khi tinh lai.

🔹 PMT – Tien tra/gui moi ky

So tien co dinh ban phai tra hoac gui moi thang/nam.

🔹 IPMT – Tien lai tung ky

Cho biet moi ky ban dang tra bao nhieu tien lai.

di lam: Thang dau tra gop → lai chiem phan lon, goc rat it

🔹 NPER – So ky (bao lau thi xong)

Tra loi cau hoi: Mat bao lau moi tra het no / dat muc tieu?

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau?

① Xac dinh von / khoan vay (PV)
② Xac dinh so tien tra duoc moi ky (PMT)
③ Tinh lai tung ky (IPMT)
④ Tinh thoi gian hoan thanh (NPER)
⑤ So voi thu nhap & rui ro
⑥ Quyet dinh: lam hay dung
Chi nen thuc hien khi:
✔ PMT ≤ 30–40% thu nhap
✔ IPMT giam dan theo thoi gian
✔ Thi truong & cong viec on dinh
Khong nen lam khi:
❌ Lai cao + thu nhap bap benh
❌ NPER qua dai → ket von

4.2 Nhap tinh huong thuc te

📌 Truong hop: Ca nhan

Uu tien NPER ngan → giam ap luc song, khong de lai an mon thu nhap.

📌 Truong hop: Doanh nghiep

Quan tam IPMT → neu lai chiem ty trong lon → du an rui ro.

📌 Truong hop: Rui ro cao

Lai cao + NPER dai → KHONG NEN THUC HIEN.
