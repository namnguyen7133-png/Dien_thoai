📘 PV & PMT – Gia tri thoi gian cua tien

Ung dung Excel TCDN1 – Chuong 2 (Quang Trung TV)

2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (giai thich doi song)

PV – Present Value (Gia tri hien tai)

PV tra loi: “Muon co X tien trong tuong lai thi hom nay can bao nhieu?”

Cong thuc Excel: =PV(rate, nper, pmt, fv)

Vi du doi song:
  • Muon co 300 trieu sau 10 nam → hom nay chi can ~170 trieu
  • Tien tuong lai bi mat gia vi lai suat & lam phat

PMT – Payment (Dong tien dinh ky)

PMT tra loi: “Moi thang/nam phai tra hoac gui bao nhieu?”

Excel: =PMT(rate, nper, pv)

⚠ Sai lam pho bien: nhin tong tien, khong nhin PMT → thieu tien hang thang → pha san

Quy tac dau (song con)

  • Tien chi ra → AM (-)
  • Tien nhan ve → DUONG (+)

2.2 Dung cho ca nhan hay cong viec?

Truong hop Nen dung Vi sao?

Doanh nghiep PV dinh gia du an, tai san
✔ Ca nhan → hoi “moi thang chiu noi khong?” ✔ Cong ty → hoi “gia tri thuc hom nay la bao nhieu?”

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (dung tu duy bai giang)

Buoc 1: Xac dinh muc tieu

  • Muon co tien tuong lai → dung PV
  • Muon biet tra bao nhieu moi ky → dung PMT

Buoc 2: Chuan hoa thoi gian

  • Tra theo thang → lai suat /12, so nam ×12
  • Khong dong nhat → ket qua sai

Buoc 3: So voi thu nhap thuc te

Neu PMT > 40% thu nhap → cuc ky rui ro

Khi nao NEN lam?

  • Thu nhap on dinh
  • Lai suat thap
  • Dong tien du

Khi nao KHONG nen?

  • Thu nhap bap benh
  • Lai suat tha noi cao
  • Phai vay de tra PMT
⚠ Co lai dai han nhung chet ngan han = that bai
