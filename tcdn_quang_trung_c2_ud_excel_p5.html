2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (giai thich doi song)

Gia tri thoi gian cua tien

Tien hom nay luon gia tri hon tien trong tuong lai vi co the dau tu sinh loi.

Vi du:
  • 1 trieu hom nay gui ngan hang → nam sau thanh 1,1 trieu
  • 1 trieu nhan sau 1 nam → da mat co hoi sinh lai

FV – Future Value (Gia tri tuong lai)

Dung khi hoi: “Sau nay toi co bao nhieu tien?”

Excel: =FV(rate, nper, pmt, pv)

PV – Present Value (Gia tri hien tai)

Dung khi hoi: “So tien tuong lai nay hom nay dang bao nhieu?”

PMT – Payment (Khoan tra deu)

Dung khi hoi: “Moi ky phai tra bao nhieu tien?”

⚠ PMT la thuoc do ap luc song con, khong phai loi nhuan

NPER – Number of Periods

Dung khi hoi: “Bao lau thi tra xong no / dat muc tieu?”

Quy uoc dau dong tien

  • Tien chi ra → AM (-)
  • Tien nhan vao → DUONG (+)

2.2 Dung cho cong viec hay ca nhan?

Hoan canh Nen dung de lam gi?
Ca nhan PMT Xem co du tien tra gop khong
Gia dinh PV + PMT Mua nha, mua xe an toan
Nguoi thu nhap cao FV Tu do tai chinh
Doanh nghiep PV danh gia du an
Startup PMT + NPER Tranh chet vi dong tien
✔ Ca nhan → quan tam PMT ✔ Cong ty → quan tam PV ✔ Lam giau dai han → quan tam FV

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (tu duy ra quyet dinh)

Buoc 1: Xac dinh van de

  • Vay tien? → PMT
  • dau tu? → FV
  • So sanh phuong an? → PV

Buoc 2: Chuan hoa du lieu

  • Lai suat & so ky cung don vi (thang hoac nam)
  • Hieu ro minh nhan tien hay tra tien

Buoc 3: danh gia dieu kien thi truong

  • Lai suat cao → han che vay dai han
  • Thu nhap khong on dinh → PMT phai thap
  • Doanh nghiep rui ro → tranh don bay
⚠ PMT > 50% dong tien → nguy co pha san
