2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc (hieu bang doi song)

Dong tien deu

La cac khoan thu hoac chi bang nhau lap lai theo thoi gian.

Vi du doi song:
  • Tra gop nha: moi thang tra 12 trieu
  • Gui tiet kiem: moi thang gui 2 trieu
  • Luong co dinh hang thang

PMT – Payment

Tinh so tien phai tra deu moi ky (goc + lai).

Excel: =PMT(rate, nper, pv, [fv], [type])

⚠ PMT cho biet AP LUC DONG TIEN, khong chi lai suat

PV – Present Value

Quy doi cac khoan tien deu trong tuong lai ve gia tri hom nay.

FV – Future Value

Tinh tong tien tich luy trong tuong lai khi gui deu.

Type (Thoi diem tra)

  • 0: Tra cuoi ky (pho bien)
  • 1: Tra dau ky (co loi hon vi huong lai som)

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (tu duy ra quyet dinh)

Buoc 1: Xac dinh dong tien

  • Nhan tien → dau +
  • Tra tien → dau -

Buoc 2: Hoi dung cau hoi

  • Moi thang tra bao nhieu? → PMT
  • Gia tri hom nay la bao nhieu? → PV
  • Tuong lai co bao nhieu tien? → FV

Buoc 3: danh gia dieu kien thi truong

  • Lai suat cao → han che vay dai han
  • Thu nhap khong on dinh → tranh PMT lon
  • Thi truong rui ro → giam don bay
⚠ PMT vuot 40–50% thu nhap → nguy co pha san
