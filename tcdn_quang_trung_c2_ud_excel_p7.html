2.1 Thuat ngu tai chinh & cong thuc kho (hieu bang doi song)

Dong tien khong deu

La cac khoan thu/chi khong bang nhau theo tung nam

Vi du doi song: Nam dau kinh doanh kho → tra it Nam sau ban tot → tra nhieu

IRR – Internal Rate of Return

La lai suat thuc te cua mot khoan vay/dau tu khi dong tien khong deu

Excel: =IRR(values)

⚠ IRR dung khi tien moi ky KHAC NHAU ❌ Khong dung RATE trong truong hop nay

NPV – Net Present Value

Dung de dua cac dong tien khac nam ve cung thoi diem (nam 0)

Excel: =NPV(rate, values)

FV – Future Value

Tinh so tien con phai tra trong tuong lai khi lai suat thay doi

doi tuong Dung ham Giai quyet viec gi

Ho kinh doanh NPV Khong vo no khi thu nhap that thuong

✔ Thu nhap cang khong deu → cang can IRR & NPV

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (theo video bai 19)

Buoc 1: Ghi dung dau dong tien

  • Nhan tien → duong (+)
  • Tra tien → am (-)

Buoc 2: Xac dinh bai toan

  • Tim lai suat → dung IRR
  • Quy ve hien tai → dung NPV

Buoc 3: Khi lai suat thay doi

Khong tinh lai tu dau → chi:

  1. Chiet khau cac khoan da tra (NPV)
  2. Tim goc con no
  3. Dung FV de tinh khoan cuoi
⚠ Lai suat tang → ap luc tra no tang rat manh
