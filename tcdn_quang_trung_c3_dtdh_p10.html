2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc tai chinh (Hieu de dung)

Dong tien chenh lech
= Dong tien may moi – Dong tien may cu

👉 Chi quan tam PHAN KHAC BIET, khong tinh toan bo doanh nghiep
Quy uoc dau
(+): Tien vao
(–): Tien ra / Tien mat di (chi phi co hoi)
NPV
NPV = Tong dong tien chiet khau – Von dau tu ban dau

NPV > 0 → NEN dau tu
📌 Video phan 10 nhan manh: “Co lai ke toan chua du – phai duong NPV”

2.2 Dung cho ca nhan hay doanh nghiep?

👤 Ca nhan
  • doi xe cu → xe moi
  • Nang cap may tinh lam viec
  • dau tu hoc ky nang
👉 Dong tien = tien tiet kiem / tien kiem them
🏢 Doanh nghiep
  • Thay may moc
  • doi day chuyen san xuat
  • dau tu cong nghe
👉 Dong tien = loi nhuan sau thue + khau hao
Nguyen tac: Khong tao dong tien → KHONG ap dung NPV

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau? (Theo dung video)

BUOC 1: Liet ke day du cac dong tien
  • Von dau tu may moi
  • Thu ban may cu (nam 0)
  • Dong tien may cu (am – chi phi co hoi)
  • Dong tien may moi (duong)
  • Thanh ly cuoi ky
BUOC 2: Lap bang dong tien theo nam (0 → n)
BUOC 3: Tinh NPV
  • NPV > 0 → lam
  • NPV < 0 → dung
BUOC 4: Neu chua chac → tim LOI NHUAN TOI THIEU
❌ KHONG lam neu:
  • Thi truong bat on
  • Dong tien khong chac chan
  • Lo toi da vuot suc chiu dung
