Giai thich – Ap dung – Quyet dinh – Tranh pha san

2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc tai chinh (Giai thich de hieu)

Von dau tu ban dau: Tien bo ra luc dau (tien mat, vay, cong suc quy doi tien).
Dong tien (Cash Flow): Tien that vao – ra, KHONG phai loi nhuan tren giay.
Thoi gian hoan von (PP): Bao lau lay lai du tien da bo ra.
Loi nhuan: Tien du ra sau khi da hoan von va tru chi phi.
❗ di lam – kinh doanh chet nhieu nhat vi: co loi nhuan nhung khong co tien mat

2.2 Cong viec & Ca nhan – Dung cai nao?

CA NHAN:
✔ PP → biet bao lau song duoc
✔ Dong tien → tra tien nha, an uong
DOANH NGHIEP:
✔ PP → tranh gay von
✔ NPV/IRR → chon du an lon
📌 Khi tien it – rui ro cao → UU TIEN PP
📌 Khi tien manh – dai han → dung NPV, IRR

3.2 Thoi diem lam gi truoc – sau?

Giai doan 1 – Thi truong xau / rui ro cao
→ Chi lam du an PP ngan (≤ 2 nam)
Giai doan 2 – On dinh
→ Mo rong, tai dau tu
Giai doan 3 – Thuan loi
→ Chap nhan PP dai de an lon
⛔ Khong du tien du phong → khong choi du an hoan von cham
