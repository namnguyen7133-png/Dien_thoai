📈 NPV – GIA TRI HIEN TAI THUAN

Hieu – Tinh – Ung dung – Tranh dau tu sai lam

2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc NPV (giai thich de hieu)

Dong tien dau tu (CF₀):
Tien bo ra luc dau (may moc + von luu dong). Luon la so am.
Dong tien thuan (CFₜ):
Tien that thu ve moi nam sau thue (KHONG phai loi nhuan ke toan).
Ty suat chiet khau (r):
Chi phi von / lai suat ky vong / muc rui ro.
Cong thuc NPV:
NPV = CF₀ + Σ CFₜ / (1+r)ᵗ
📌 NPV > 0 → Du an lam ban GIAU LEN
📌 NPV < 0 → Du an lam ban NGHEO dI

2.2 Ca nhan & Doanh nghiep – dung cai nao?

CA NHAN:
✔ Mua nha tra gop → NPV
✔ dau tu hoc nghe dai han → NPV
DOANH NGHIEP:
✔ dau tu nha xuong, day chuyen → NPV (bat buoc)
✔ So du an lon → chon NPV cao nhat
⛔ Khong dung NPV khi ban khong chiu noi thieu tien ngan han

3.2 Lam gi truoc – sau? Khi nao moi nen dau tu?

Thi truong xau / rui ro cao:
→ Chi chon du an NPV duong & thu tien som
Thi truong on dinh:
→ Chap nhan NPV duong nhung tien ve cham
Thuan loi:
→ dau tu dai han, NPV rat lon
⛔ Du an NPV duong nhung khong du tien song → van chet
