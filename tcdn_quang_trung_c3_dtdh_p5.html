📊 IRR – TY SUAT DOANH LOI NOI BO

Hieu ban chat – Tranh bay % – Ra quyet dinh dung

2.1 Thuat ngu IRR – giai thich de hieu

IRR (Internal Rate of Return):
La muc lai suat lam cho NPV = 0.
→ Hieu don gian: du an tu sinh loi bao nhieu %/nam.
Chi phi su dung von (r):
Lai vay ngan hang / ty suat sinh loi ky vong.
Nguyen tac cot loi:
IRR > r → Du an dang lam
IRR < r → Du an lam ban ngheo di
📌 IRR KHONG cho ban biet loi bao nhieu tien –
📌 IRR chi cho biet ty le % sinh loi

2.2 Ca nhan & Doanh nghiep – dung IRR khi nao?

CA NHAN:
✔ So sanh cac kenh dau tu (%/nam)
✔ Goi bao hiem – chung chi quy – sua nha cho thue
DOANH NGHIEP:
✔ So sanh du an cung quy mo von
✔ Chi dung IRR kem NPV
⛔ Khong dung IRR mot minh khi:
– Quy mo von khac nhau
– Dong tien doi dau nhieu lan

3.2 Lam gi truoc – sau? Khi nao IRR moi co y nghia?

Buoc 1: Kiem tra dong tien co hop ly khong
Buoc 2: Tinh NPV truoc – xem co duong khong
Buoc 3: So IRR voi chi phi von
⛔ IRR cao nhung NPV thap → bay %
⛔ IRR cao nhung von nho → khong lam giau duoc
