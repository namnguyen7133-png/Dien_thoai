2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc (Giai thich doi song)

Profitability Index (PI)
➡ Moi 1 dong von bo ra tao duoc bao nhieu dong gia tri
➡ Giong nhu: Bo 10 trieu → thu ve tuong duong bao nhieu trieu hom nay?
Cong thuc PI
PI = Gia tri hien tai dong tien vao / Von dau tu ban dau
📌 Khac NPV: NPV → lai bao nhieu tien PI → lai hieu suat bao nhieu lan

2.2 Dung cai nao cho ai?

  • Ca nhan: hoc nghe, mua khoa hoc, mua xe chay dich vu → dung PI
  • Gia dinh: sua nha cho thue → dung PI + NPV
  • Doanh nghiep: nhieu du an, von han che → uu tien PI cao nhat
✔ Von it → dung PI ✔ Muon biet lai tuyet doi → dung NPV

3.2 Lam luc nao? dieu kien moi duoc lam

🔁 Trinh tu chuan

  1. Xac dinh von bo ra (ban dau)
  2. Du bao dong tien vao tung nam
  3. Chon lai suat chiet khau (rui ro)
  4. Tinh PI
  5. So sanh PI voi 1

📊 dieu kien thi truong

  • Thi truong on dinh → PI ≥ 1.1 moi lam
  • Rui ro cao → PI ≥ 1.3 moi lam
  • PI sat 1 → KHONG LAM
⚠ Du an PI cao nhung von nho → chua chac tot bang PI vua nhung quy mo lon

4.2 Nhap tinh huong & Giai quyet

📌 Chon tinh huong mau

📍 Case 1 – Ca nhan hoc nghe

Hoc phi 30 trieu → thu nhap tang them 10 trieu/nam trong 5 nam → PI > 1 ⇒ NEN HOC

📍 Case 2 – Gia dinh mua xe chay dich vu

Von 500 trieu – dong tien bap benh → PI sat 1 ⇒ RUI RO CAO

📍 Case 3 – Doanh nghiep dau tu may moc

So sanh nhieu may → chon may co PI cao nhat
