2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc (Giai thich doi song)

Van de tuoi tho khac nhau
➡ Khong the so du an 3 nam voi 6 nam bang NPV truc tiep ➡ Giong nhu so dien thoai dung 2 nam voi may dung 5 nam
EAA (Equivalent Annual Annuity)
➡ Loi ich binh quan moi nam sau khi da quy doi thoi gian ➡ Tra loi cau hoi: “Moi nam du an nuoi ta bao nhieu tien?”
Cong thuc
EAA = NPV / PVFA
PVFA = [1 − (1+r)−n] / r
📌 EAA giup so sanh cong bang cac du an co thoi gian song khac nhau

2.2 Dung trong cong viec & ca nhan

  • Ca nhan: mua do ben – thue hay mua → dung EAA
  • Gia dinh: thue nha dai han hay mua tra gop → EAA
  • Doanh nghiep: chon may moc tuoi tho khac nhau → BAT BUOC EAA
✔ Khi du an co the lap lai → luon dung EAA ❌ Khong dung IRR trong truong hop nay

3.2 Lam luc nao? dieu kien moi duoc lam

🔁 Trinh tu chuan

  1. Tinh NPV tung du an
  2. Xac dinh tuoi tho rieng
  3. Tinh PVFA theo tung tuoi tho
  4. Tinh EAA
  5. So sanh EAA

📊 dieu kien thi truong

  • Thi truong on dinh → chon EAA cao
  • Rui ro cong nghe → uu tien du an ngan han (EAA cao, n nho)
  • Thieu von → chon EAA giup quay vong nhanh
⚠ NPV cao nhung EAA thap → von bi “chon” lau

4.2 Nhap tinh huong & giai quyet

📌 Chon tinh huong mau

📍 Case 1 – Ca nhan

May cu: re – dung 2 nam May moi: dat – dung 5 nam ➡ Tinh EAA chi phi/nam → chon cai ton it moi nam hon

📍 Case 2 – Gia dinh

Thue 10 nam vs mua tra gop 20 nam ➡ So EAA dong tien ra hang nam

📍 Case 3 – Doanh nghiep

May A: NPV cao – tuoi tho dai May B: NPV thap – quay vong nhanh ➡ Chon may co EAA cao hon
