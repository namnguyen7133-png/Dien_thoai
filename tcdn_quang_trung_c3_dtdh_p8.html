2.1 Thuat ngu – Cong thuc – Ap dung doi song

Dong tien chenh lech
➡ So sanh CHI PHAN KHAC NHAU giua:
  • Mua may moi
  • Tiep tuc dung may cu
Chi phi co hoi
➡ Thu nhap tu may cu ma ban mat di neu ban may cu ➡ Phai dua vao dong tien ra
Thue khi ban may cu
Lai → nop thue Lo → duoc giam thue ➡ Tien thu thuc = gia ban
Cong thuc quyet dinh
NPV chenh lech = PV(dong tien may moi – may cu)
NPV > 0 → thay may
📌 day la bai toan “co lai nhung van KHONG nen lam”

2.2 Khi nao dung cho cong viec – ca nhan

  • Ca nhan: ban dien thoai / laptop cu de mua may moi
  • Gia dinh: doi xe xang sang xe dien
  • Doanh nghiep: thay day chuyen, robot, may CNC
✔ Dung khi co tai san dang hoat dong ❌ Khong dung NPV tong – phai dung NPV chenh lech

3.2 Trinh tu – Thoi diem – dieu kien thi truong

🔁 Trinh tu chuan

  1. Phan tich may cu (thu nhap – thanh ly – thue)
  2. Phan tich may moi (dau tu – dong tien – thanh ly)
  3. Lap bang dong tien chenh lech
  4. Tinh NPV
  5. Ra quyet dinh

📊 dieu kien nen thay

  • Chi phi bao tri may cu cao
  • May moi tang nang suat, tiet kiem chi phi
  • Doanh nghiep co dong tien on dinh

⚠ Rui ro khong nen thay

  • NPV chenh lech am
  • Von lon – thu hoi cham
  • Cong nghe moi chua on dinh
💡 Lai ke toan tang = gia tri DN tang

4.2 Nhap tinh huong – Tinh nhanh NPV chenh lech

📌 Chon tinh huong mau

📍 Case 1 – Ca nhan

Ban dien thoai cu 5 trieu de mua may moi 20 trieu ➡ Tinh xem phan chenh lech co dang hay khong

📍 Case 2 – Gia dinh

Xe cu ton xang – xe dien dau tu lon ➡ So chi phi tiet kiem hang nam

📍 Case 3 – Doanh nghiep

May cu van chay nhung hao dien ➡ May moi dau tu lon nhung giam chi phi van hanh
