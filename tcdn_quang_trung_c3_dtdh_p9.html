📘 TAI CHINH DOANH NGHIEP – HIEU & AP DUNG THUC TE

Danh cho ca nhan – di lam – kinh doanh – dau tu

2.1 Thuat ngu & Cong thuc tai chinh (Giai thich de hieu)

NPV (Gia tri hien tai rong)
NPV = Tong dong tien hien tai – Von dau tu ban dau

👉 Y nghia:
  • NPV > 0 → NEN lam
  • NPV < 0 → KHONG nen lam
Dong tien
= Loi nhuan sau thue + Khau hao

👉 Vi khau hao khong lam tien di ra khoi tui
Chi phi co hoi
La so tien ban mat di khi chon phuong an A thay vi B

👉 Vi du: Giu may cu la mat co hoi ban may lay tien dau tu khac

2.2 Dung trong cong viec hay ca nhan?

👤 Ca nhan:
  • doi xe cu hay sua?
  • Hoc ky nang moi co dang tien?
  • Mua nha hay thue?
🏢 Doanh nghiep:
  • Thay may cu hay giu?
  • Mo chi nhanh moi?
  • dau tu cong nghe?
👉 Nguyen tac: Neu co dong tien → dung NPV Neu khong co dong tien → dung, khong lam

3.2 Lam gi truoc – lam gi sau?

BUOC 1: Xac dinh von bo ra ban dau
BUOC 2: Uoc luong dong tien hang nam
BUOC 3: Xem rui ro thi truong
  • Thi truong xau → khong dau tu
  • Dong tien khong on dinh → khong dau tu
BUOC 4: Tinh lai – lo – diem hoa von
👉 Chi lam khi:
  • Lai du lon
  • Chiu duoc lo xau nhat
  • Con tien song neu that bai
