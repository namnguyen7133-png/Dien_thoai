2.1 Giai thich thuat ngu tai chinh (Hieu theo doi song)

1. Tai san luu dong (TSLd)

dinh nghia: Tai san quay vong trong ≤ 1 nam.

doi song: Tien trong vi, hang ban online, nguoi khac dang no ban.

2. Vong quay hang ton kho

Cong thuc:

Vong quay HTK = Gia von hang ban / HTK binh quan

Y nghia: Ban nhanh hay cham. Quay cham = tien chet.

3. Ky thu tien trung binh

Cong thuc:

Ky thu tien = 360 / Vong quay khoan phai thu

doi song: Cho ban vay 30 ngay hay 3 thang?

2.2 Nen dung cho ca nhan hay cong viec?

  • Ca nhan: Quan ly tien mat, cho vay, xoay von ca nhan.
  • di lam: Quan ly ton kho, cong no khach hang.
  • Doanh nghiep: Quyet dinh nhap hang – ban – thu tien.
Nguyen tac: Neu ban co tien ra – tien vao → dung kien thuc nay.

3.2 Trinh tu lam dung (Khong lam nguoc)

Buoc 1: danh gia dieu kien thi truong

  • Nhu cau co that khong?
  • Ban nhanh hay cham?

Buoc 2: Tinh von can thiet

Von = HTK + Phai thu – Phai tra

Buoc 3: Uoc tinh lai – lo

  • Lai = Doanh thu – Gia von – Chi phi
  • Thua lo xay ra khi vong quay cham

Buoc 4: Quyet dinh lam hay khong

  • Vong quay nhanh → lam
  • Thu tien cham → dung
