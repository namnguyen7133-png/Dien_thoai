Phan 3: Cong Thuc Bi Mat Trong Cashflow Game

Cong Thuc 1: Ty Le Loi Nhuan Tren Von dau Tu (ROI)

ROI = (Cashflow hang thang / Down Payment) * 12 * 100% (de tinh % hang nam). Phan loai: <4% trung binh, 4-5% tot, >5% tot hon.

Thuat toan hoan thien: ROI hang nam = (Cashflow / Down Payment) * 12 * 100. danh gia dua tren nguong trong video.

Cong Thuc 2: So Luong Tien Mat Can Thiet de dat Tu Do Tai Chinh

Tien mat can = Chi phi hang thang (Thu nhap thu dong can) / (ROI mong muon / 100). Muc tieu: Thu nhap thu dong > Chi phi.

Thuat toan hoan thien: Tien mat = Expenses / (ROI / 100). Dua tren vi du: 6000$ / 0.05 = 120000$.
