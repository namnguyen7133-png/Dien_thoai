Chuyen doi Tieng Viet sang So Thu Tu Tieng Han & Tieng Phap

Nhap van ban tieng Viet, chuong trinh se do tim trong bang chu cai va hien thi so thu tu theo quy tac da cho

🇰🇷 Ket qua tieng Han:

Chua co ket qua

🇫🇷 Ket qua tieng Phap:

Chua co ket qua

Quy tac chuyen doi:

Voi moi ky tu tieng Viet nhap vao, chuong trinh se:

  1. Tim trong bang chu cai de xac dinh so thu tu cua chu cai co am tuong tu
  2. Neu so thu tu lon hon 9, chia cho 9 va lay phan du
  3. Neu ket qua la 0, lay gia tri 9

Vi du tieng Han: Chu "a" (ㅏ) co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du tieng Han: Chu "k" (ㄱ) co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du tieng Phap: Chu "a" co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du tieng Phap: Chu "k" co so thu tu la 11 → 11 > 9 nen 11 % 9 = 2 → Ket qua: 2

🇰🇷 Bang chu cai tieng Han (Hangul) va am tieng Viet tuong tu

Thu tu Chu cai Han Quoc Ten goi Am tieng Viet tuong tu

🇫🇷 Bang chu cai tieng Phap va am tieng Viet tuong tu

Thu tu Chu cai tieng Phap Am tieng Viet tuong tu

So sanh dac diem bang chu cai

Ngon ngu He chu So luong dac diem noi bat
Tieng Han Hangul 40 ky tu (19 phu am, 21 nguyen am) Chu viet khoa hoc, ghep van thanh khoi
Tieng Phap Latinh 26 chu cai + dau phu Co dau trong am, phat am khac voi viet
Tieng Viet Latinh (Quoc ngu) 29 chu cai + dau thanh 6 thanh dieu, nhieu dau phu

Luu y quan trong:

Tieng Han su dung he thong chu Hangul gom cac phu am va nguyen am ghep lai thanh khoi. Tieng Phap co cac ky tu dac biet voi dau nhu e, e, e, c.

Vi du ve ghep chu Han: ㅎ (h) + ㅏ (a) + ㄴ (n) = 한 (han)

Cac ky tu dac biet, dau cau va khoang trang se duoc bo qua trong qua trinh chuyen doi.
