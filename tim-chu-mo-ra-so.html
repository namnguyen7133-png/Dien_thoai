📡 Quy tac chuyen doi:

Voi moi ky tu tieng Viet nhap vao, chuong trinh se:

  1. Chuyen doi sang ma Morse tuong ung
  2. dem tong so dau cham (•) va gach ngang (−) trong ma Morse
  3. Lay tong so dau lam so thu tu
  4. Neu so thu tu lon hon 9, chia cho 9 va lay phan du
  5. Neu ket qua la 0, lay gia tri 9

Vi du: Chu "A" co ma Morse "•−" (1 cham + 1 gach = 2) → Ket qua: 2

Vi du: Chu "S" co ma Morse "•••" (3 cham = 3) → Ket qua: 3

Vi du: Chu "X" co ma Morse "−••−" (2 cham + 2 gach = 4) → Ket qua: 4

⚠ Luu y quan trong:

Ma Morse su dung hai ky tu co ban: dau cham (•) va dau gach ngang (−).

Thoi luong: Dau cham = 1 don vi, Dau gach = 3 don vi

Khoang cach: Giua cac dau = 1 don vi, Giua cac chu = 3 don vi, Giua cac tu = 7 don vi

Cac ky tu dac biet, dau cau va khoang trang se duoc xu ly theo quy tac ma Morse.
