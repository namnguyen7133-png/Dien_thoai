Chuyen doi Tieng Viet sang So Thu Tu Tieng Nhat & Lien minh chau Au

Nhap van ban tieng Viet, chuong trinh se do tim trong bang chu cai va hien thi so thu tu theo quy tac da cho

🇯🇵 Ket qua tieng Nhat:

Chua co ket qua

🇪🇺 Ket qua Lien minh chau Au:

Chua co ket qua

📌 Quy tac chuyen doi:

Voi moi ky tu tieng Viet nhap vao, chuong trinh se:

  1. Tim trong bang chu cai de xac dinh so thu tu cua chu cai co am tuong tu
  2. Neu so thu tu lon hon 9, chia cho 9 va lay phan du
  3. Neu ket qua la 0, lay gia tri 9

Vi du tieng Nhat: Chu "a" (あ) co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du tieng Nhat: Chu "ka" (か) co so thu tu la 6 → Ket qua: 6

Vi du Lien minh chau Au: Chu "a" co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du Lien minh chau Au: Chu "k" co so thu tu la 11 → 11 > 9 nen 11 % 9 = 2 → Ket qua: 2

🇪🇺 Ve Lien minh chau Au (EU):

Lien minh chau Au gom 27 quoc gia thanh vien, su dung da ngon ngu. Ung dung nay su dung bang chu cai Latinh tieu chuan cua EU, ket hop tu cac ngon ngu chinh nhu:

  • Tieng Anh, Tieng Phap, Tieng duc, Tieng Y, Tieng Tay Ban Nha
  • Bao gom cac ky tu dac biet tu nhieu ngon ngu chau Au
  • Ho tro da ngon ngu va ky tu dac biet

⚠ Luu y quan trong:

Tieng Nhat su dung 3 he thong chu: Hiragana (chu mem), Katakana (chu cung), va Kanji (Han tu). Ung dung nay su dung bang Hiragana co ban.

Lien minh chau Au bao gom nhieu ngon ngu khac nhau, bang chu cai EU la tap hop cac ky tu Latinh pho bien nhat.

Cac ky tu dac biet, dau cau va khoang trang se duoc bo qua trong qua trinh chuyen doi.

ℹ Thong tin chi tiet ve he thong chu viet

🇯🇵 He thong chu Nhat Ban

Hiragana (ひらかな): 46 ky tu, dung cho tu thuan Nhat, tro tu, bien to dong tu.

Katakana (カタカナ): 46 ky tu, dung cho tu muon nuoc ngoai, ten rieng.

Kanji (漢字): Chu Han, khoang 2000 chu thong dung.

Romaji: Phien am Latinh, dung cho nguoi nuoc ngoai hoc tieng Nhat.

🇪🇺 da ngon ngu Lien minh chau Au

Ngon ngu chinh thuc: 24 ngon ngu tai EU.

He chu pho bien: Latinh (20 ngon ngu), Cyrillic, Greek.

Ky tu dac biet: c, n, a, æ, ø, ß, va nhieu ky tu co dau.

Chinh sach ngon ngu: da ngu, binh dang ngon ngu.

🇻🇳 Chu Quoc ngu Viet Nam

Lich su: Do cac giao si phuong Tay va hoc gia Viet Nam phat trien.

dac diem: Dua tren bang chu Latinh, them dau thanh va dau phu.

Thanh dieu: 6 thanh (ngang, huyen, sac, hoi, nga, nang).

Uu diem: De hoc, ghi am chinh xac tieng Viet.
