Chuyen doi Tieng Viet sang So Thu Tu Tieng Trung & Tieng duc

Nhap van ban tieng Viet, chuong trinh se do tim trong bang chu cai va hien thi so thu tu theo quy tac da cho

🇨🇳 Ket qua tieng Trung:

Chua co ket qua

🇩🇪 Ket qua tieng duc:

Chua co ket qua

Quy tac chuyen doi:

Voi moi ky tu tieng Viet nhap vao, chuong trinh se:

  1. Tim trong bang chu cai de xac dinh so thu tu cua chu cai co am tuong tu
  2. Neu so thu tu lon hon 9, chia cho 9 va lay phan du
  3. Neu ket qua la 0, lay gia tri 9

Vi du tieng duc: Chu "a" co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

Vi du tieng duc: Chu "k" co so thu tu la 11 → 11 > 9 nen 11 % 9 = 2 → Ket qua: 2

Vi du tieng Trung: Chu "a" (啊) co so thu tu la 1 → Ket qua: 1

🇨🇳 Bang chu cai tieng Trung (Pinyin) va am tieng Viet tuong tu

Thu tu Chu cai tieng Trung (Pinyin) Chu Han vi du Am tieng Viet tuong tu

🇩🇪 Bang chu cai tieng duc va am tieng Viet tuong tu

Thu tu Chu cai tieng duc Am tieng Viet tuong tu

Luu y quan trong:

Am tuong duong chi mang tinh gan dung. Tieng Trung su dung he thong chu Han va phien am Pinyin. Tieng duc co mot so ky tu dac biet nhu a, o, u, ß.

Cac ky tu dac biet, dau cau va khoang trang se duoc bo qua trong qua trinh chuyen doi.
