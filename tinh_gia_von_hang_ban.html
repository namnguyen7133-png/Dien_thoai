4 Phuong Phap Tinh Gia Von Hang Ban

1. FIFO (First-In, First-Out) - Nhap truoc xuat truoc

Nguyen tac: Hang nhap kho truoc thi xuat ban truoc.

Uu diem: Phan anh dung dong chay vat chat cua hang hoa, gia von on dinh khi gia ca on dinh.

Nhuoc diem: Trong thoi ky lam phat, loi nhuan co the bi phong dai.

2. LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) - Nhap sau xuat truoc

Nguyen tac: Hang nhap kho sau cung thi xuat ban truoc.

Uu diem: Phu hop trong thoi ky lam phat, gia von phan anh gia hien tai, thue thu nhap thap hon.

Nhuoc diem: Khong phan anh dung dong chay vat chat, gia tri ton kho co the loi thoi.

3. Gia Binh Quan Gia Quyen

Nguyen tac: Tinh gia trung binh cua tat ca hang hoa trong kho tai thoi diem xuat.

Uu diem: don gian, de tinh toan, gia von on dinh, phu hop voi hang hoa dong nhat.

Nhuoc diem: Khong phan anh bien dong gia thi truong kip thoi.

4. Gia dich Danh

Nguyen tac: Xac dinh chinh xac gia nhap cua tung mat hang cu the khi xuat ban.

Uu diem: Chinh xac tuyet doi, phu hop voi hang hoa co gia tri cao, khac biet ro rang.

Nhuoc diem: Ton nhieu cong suc theo doi, chi ap dung duoc voi hang hoa co the nhan dien rieng biet.

Luu Y Quan Trong

Moi cach tinh se cho ra bao cao loi nhuan khac nhau trong ngan han nhung khong lam thay doi tong gia tri tai san tong the cua doanh nghiep tren bang can doi ke toan.

Viec lua chon phuong phap tinh gia von phu thuoc vao:

  • dac diem hang hoa (de hu hong, gia tri cao/thap, dong nhat/khac biet)
  • Tinh hinh gia ca thi truong (lam phat, giam phat, on dinh)
  • Muc tieu quan ly tai chinh (toi thieu thue, on dinh loi nhuan, v.v.)
  • Quy dinh ke toan va thue cua quoc gia
