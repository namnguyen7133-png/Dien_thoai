Tom tat:
• 3 nguoi an chung 1 bat com
• 4 nguoi an chung 1 dia ca muoi
• Tong so bat dia don ra la 301 cai
• Hoi: Co bao nhieu nguoi lam nha, bao nhieu bat com va bao nhieu dia ca muoi?

May Tinh Giai Toan

Nhap tong so bat dia de giai bai toan (hoac dung gia tri mac dinh 301):

Cach 1: Giai bang phuong trinh dai so

day la cach giai toan hoc chinh thong, dung phuong trinh de tim nghiem.

Cach 2: Giai dan gian bang quy tac tam suat

Phuong phap dan gian dung ti le va quy tac tam suat de tinh toan.

Cach 3: Giai dan gian bang chia nhom

Phuong phap chia thanh cac nhom co ti le co dinh de tinh toan.

Ket Qua Bai Toan

0 So Nguoi
0 Bat Com
0 dia Ca Muoi

Tong so bat dia: 0 (kiem tra: 0)

Chi Tiet 3 Cach Giai

1. Cach giai bang lap phuong trinh (01:46)

Goi so nguoi la x, so bat com la b, so dia ca la c.

Ta co: b = x/3, c = x/4 va b + c = 301

Phuong trinh: x/3 + x/4 = 301

Quy dong mau so chung la 12: (4x + 3x)/12 = 301

Giai ra: 7x = 301 × 12 = 3612 → x = 3612 ÷ 7 = 516

2. Cach giai dan gian 1 - Quy tac tam suat (05:45)

Tim boi so chung nho nhat cua 3 va 4 la 12.

Xet 1 nhom 12 nguoi: dung 4 bat com (12÷3) va 3 dia ca (12÷4).

Tong bat dia cho 12 nguoi: 4 + 3 = 7

Ap dung quy tac tam suat: 12 nguoi → 7 bat dia, vay ? nguoi → 301 bat dia

So nguoi = (12 × 301) ÷ 7 = 3612 ÷ 7 = 516

3. Cach giai dan gian 2 - Chia theo nhom (06:52)

Cung xet nhom 12 nguoi dung 7 bat dia.

So nhom = 301 ÷ 7 = 43 nhom

So nguoi = 43 × 12 = 516 nguoi

So bat com = 43 × 4 = 172 bat

So dia ca = 43 × 3 = 129 dia

Tai Sao Co 3 Cach Giai?

Bai toan co nay minh hoa cho su linh hoat trong tu duy toan hoc:

  • Cach 1 la phuong phap dai so chinh thong, dung cho giai toan hoc thuat.
  • Cach 2 va 3 la phuong phap dan gian, dung ti le va nhom de tinh nham nhanh ma khong can phuong trinh phuc tap.
  • Ca 3 cach deu cho cung mot ket qua, chung to toan hoc co nhieu con duong de di den dap an dung.
