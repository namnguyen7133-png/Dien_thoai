🌿 Ngu hanh vuong – Trieu chung & thuc pham

Tra nhanh theo trieu chung, bo phan, mon an
🔍

🌳 Moc vuong

Gan – Mat
⚡ Trieu chung:
De cau gat, dau dau, chong mat, dau nua dau, u tai, dau tuc ha suon, mat do, huyet ap dao dong.
🧬 Bo phan lien quan:
Gan, mat, mat, gan, mong tay, than kinh.
🥗 Thuc pham can bang:
  • Rau can (canh, xao toi)
  • Bi dao (nau tom, nau nam)
  • Rau ma (sinh to, an song)
  • dau phu (canh chua, sot ca)
  • Rau muong luoc

🔥 Hoa vuong

Tim – Mach
⚡ Trieu chung:
Mat ngu, tran troc, tim hoi hop, mieng kho luoi do, nhiet mieng, nuoc tieu vang, tao bon, mat do.
🧬 Bo phan lien quan:
Tim, mach mau, luoi, da mat.
🥗 Thuc pham can bang:
  • Bi dao (nau canh, ep nuoc)
  • Rau den (luoc, nau cua)
  • dau xanh (che, nuoc uong)
  • Cu sen (canh, mut sen)
  • Rau day (canh cua)

🌍 Tho vuong

Ty – Vi
⚡ Trieu chung:
day bung, kho tieu, o hoi, an khong ngon hoac an nhieu van met, nguoi nang ne, beo beu, mieng nhat/ngot.
🧬 Bo phan lien quan:
Ty (la lach, tuy), da day, co bap, mieng.
🥗 Thuc pham can bang:
  • Gao lut (com, nuoc rang)
  • Bi do (canh, chao, sup)
  • Khoai lang luoc
  • Gung tuoi (tra, nau canh)
  • Rau thom (kinh gioi, tia to)

⚔ Kim vuong

Phe – dai trang
⚡ Trieu chung:
Da kho, nut ne, mui kho, chay mau cam, ho khan/dom vang, viem hong, tao bon, de benh ho hap.
🧬 Bo phan lien quan:
Phoi, dai trang, da, mui.
🥗 Thuc pham can bang:
  • Le hap duong phen
  • Cu cai trang (nau canh, hap mat ong)
  • Moc nhi den (goi, xao)
  • Rau cau (rong bien) nau canh
  • Tao tau (chao, tra)

💧 Thuy vuong

Than – Bang quang
⚡ Trieu chung:
Phu thung (mat, chan tay), dau lung moi goi, so lanh, tieu nhieu lan nuoc tieu trong, nguoi ue oai.
🧬 Bo phan lien quan:
Than, bang quang, xuong, tai, toc.
🥗 Thuc pham can bang:
  • Rau ram (an kem trung vit lon)
  • Hanh la, hanh tim (nem nem)
  • dau den (nau chao, nuoc rang)
  • Rau ngo (canh chua)
  • Cai cuc (tan o nau thit bam)
⚕ Luu y: “Vuong” chi trang thai mat can bang. Thong tin mang tinh tham khao. Can tham kham bac si neu co trieu chung nghiem trong.
