KIM TU dO - CON dUONG TU DO TAI CHINH

Robert Kiyosaki chia 4 nhom nguoi kiem tien. Muc tieu: di chuyen tu ben trai (E,S) sang ben phai (B,I)

E - Employee (Nguoi lam cong)

Thu nhap: Luong, thuong

Uu diem: On dinh, an toan

Nhuoc diem: Thu nhap gioi han, phu thuoc vao cong ty

Vi du: Nhan vien van phong, cong nhan, giao vien

S - Self-employed (Tu lam chu)

Thu nhap: Tu dich vu ca nhan

Uu diem: Tu chu, thu nhap cao hon

Nhuoc diem: Lam nhieu huong nhieu, kho mo rong

Vi du: Bac si tu, luat su, chu tiem nho

B - Business Owner (Chu doanh nghiep)

Thu nhap: Loi nhuan tu he thong

Uu diem: Thu nhap thu dong, mo rong duoc

Nhuoc diem: Rui ro cao, can von lon

Vi du: Chu chuoi cua hang, nha hang, xuong san xuat

I - Investor (Nha dau tu)

Thu nhap: dau tu, co tuc, cho thue

Uu diem: Tien lam viec cho minh, tu do thoi gian

Nhuoc diem:Can kien thuc, von lon, rui ro thi truong

Vi du: Nha dau tu bat dong san, chung khoan, vang

📋 KE HOACH HANH dONG THEO TUNG O:

O E & S (Ben trai):

  1. Tiet kiem it nhat 10% thu nhap
  2. Hoc them ky nang de tang luong
  3. dau tu vao kien thuc tai chinh
  4. Bat dau kinh doanh nho ban thoi gian
  5. Mua tai san sinh loi (vang, chung khoan)

O B & I (Ben phai):

  1. Xay dung he thong kinh doanh tu dong
  2. da dang hoa nguon thu nhap
  3. Tai dau tu loi nhuan
  4. Xay dung danh muc dau tu da dang
  5. Mua tai san, tranh tieu san

Ban hien dang o o: E - Employee
