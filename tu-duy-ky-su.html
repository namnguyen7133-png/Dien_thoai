Vi du minh hoa

Tu duy ky su: Tu yeu cau den ma HTML

Gia su chung ta can tao mot card hien thi san pham voi cac yeu cau:

  • Hien thi hinh anh san pham
  • Hien thi ten, mo ta ngan va gia
  • Co nut "Them vao gio hang"
  • Responsive tren moi thiet bi
  • Co hieu ung tuong tac khi hover

Cach tiep can cua ky su:

<div class="product-card">
  <div class="product-image-container">
    <img src="product.jpg" alt="Mo ta san pham" class="product-image">
  </div>
  <div class="product-info">
    <h3 class="product-name">Ten san pham</h3>
    <p class="product-description">Mo ta ngan ve san pham</p>
    <div class="product-price">299.000d</div>
    <button class="add-to-cart-btn" aria-label="Them vao gio hang">
      <i class="fas fa-shopping-cart"></i> Them vao gio hang
    </button>
  </div>
</div>

Phan tich tu duy:

  1. Su dung class co y nghia, de hieu
  2. Tach cac phan tu thanh cac container doc lap
  3. Su dung semantic HTML (h3 cho tieu de)
  4. Them thuoc tinh aria-label de ho tro accessibility
  5. Cau truc ro rang de de dang ap dung CSS va JavaScript sau nay

Loi khuyen tu chuyen gia

Luon bat dau tu cau truc

Truoc khi viet bat ky dong ma nao, hay phac thao cau truc HTML tren giay hoac bang cong cu wireframe. dieu nay giup ban hinh dung ro rang ve nhung gi can xay dung.

Kiem tra tren nhieu moi truong

Mot ky su gioi luon kiem tra ma cua minh tren nhieu trinh duyet, thiet bi va kich thuoc man hinh khac nhau truoc khi xem la hoan thanh.

Viet ma cho nguoi khac doc

Viet ma sao cho nguoi khac (hoac chinh ban trong tuong lai) co the de dang hieu va chinh sua. Su dung comments khi can thiet va dat ten co y nghia.
